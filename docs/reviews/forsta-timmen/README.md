### Earlier points that turned out differently once the world ran

- **"Kharis 1.0 · Wrathful" with 4% divine punishment**: now "Kharis 40.0 · Indifferent", net −0.33 a game day. That matches the opening dispatch. It was a pre-start placeholder. The pre-start screen was alarming for nothing.
- **"City: No city founded yet. Click a hex to colonize" / "Economy: No settlements" / "Recruit: No capital yet"**: all of these filled in properly once the city existed.
- **"Time stands still" versus the day counter moving**: once running, the counter did advance (Day 3 at founding, Day 7 about 20 minutes later) and the sun/moon arc moves. The old contradiction is therefore real only before the start.
- **"Everything says yes on every hex"**: still true for the founding forecast, but now each catchment hex shows different goods and rates, so the differences did show up after founding.
- **Orders**: the world refusal is gone. The March popup, which was cut off at the bottom before, still sits over the hex panel.
- **The "REPORT" button as a bug form**: unchanged. What happened is still only in the bell.
- **The blank map after switching to phone**: unchanged, the ⌂ button was still needed on phone and again on desktop.
- **Map**: the island is bigger and has a sea on the east after founding. Before founding I could not know there was a coast.
