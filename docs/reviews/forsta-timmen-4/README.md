## 5. The three things that would have helped me most

1. **A first-build nudge.** The city starts with 0 stone and 0 timber, and the first thing I wanted (Farm, Barracks) said "city has 0". A line like "Put workers on stone and timber first" in the Buildings tab, and a real-time estimate ("enough stone in about 8 minutes"), would have saved me the longest wait and the longest puzzle.
2. **One food/production number that agrees everywhere.** Hex panel ("fish 1 per game day total"), Warehouse ("Fish +6"), Economy ("Food +0.7", "stocks growing", Grain "−5.6") and the City panel ("Food −0.3 ... lasts over 1,000 game days") told different stories at different moments. A single sentence ("You eat 5, you make 6.5, you are fine") would settle it.
3. **A short explanation of the "extras" that appear unasked:** the free Galley ("Poseidon grants a Galley"), the purple storm hexes (and a warning on "Send galleys →"), the ⚒ button, silver (120, no income, no use), and Kharis "Indifferent" (what are the steps?).

What worked well and should be kept: the opening dispatch with 5 steps, the confirm step before founding, the Codex "How do I…?" page, the explanatory march texts with real wall-clock arrival times, the red refusal messages ("Impassable", "Nothing sent — ..."), the Warehouse food breakdown, and the phone march screen.
