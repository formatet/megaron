Order a unit to march to a target hex (q,r coordinates).

Use --preview to estimate arrival without sending an order. The estimate uses
the current state; courier orders and unknown routes cannot promise an arrival.

Terrain passability:
  Impassable (all forces):  mountain_limestone, mountain_red
  Land forces only:         plains, hills, forest_olive_grove, forest_cedar,
                           scrub_maquis, semi_desert, river_valley, river_delta
  Naval forces only:        coastal_sea, deep_sea, river
  Both land AND naval:     river_ford (the river's port — steep move cost for
                           either, but the one hex where a land unit can
                           cross and a ship can still sail through)
  (Land forces cannot enter sea or river. Ships can dock at your coastal cities
  or harbours; other land remains impassable to naval forces.
  river is impassable to land forces — it is a wall, not a crossing. Every
  ~10 hexes of a river's length carries one river_ford instead.)

A newly recruited land cohort must finish gathering men and training before
it can march. Once ready, a cohort can still march after losing men; it does
not need to recover to 100 first.
A unit in fortify stance must be cleared (stance none) before marching.

Exploring: an explore order first travels to the exact selected hex, even
if it is already seen, then explores unseen reachable ground around it.
It records what the unit sees along its route.
Run 'keryx map' to see the frontier coordinates. Fog on the map does not
guarantee a route for this unit. If the chosen hex cannot be reached,
the order is refused; try another hex or a unit departing from elsewhere.

--intent explore goes first to the chosen hex, then explores around it for --ticks game days.
It turns home by half the duration and reports what it saw on return. It needs
a reachable city of yours to return to. A land unit in the field receives the
order by Runner; a ship must be commanded in port. Works for land or naval
forces. Omit --ticks for the server default. Before sending, the read-only forecast
validates the duration and return budget; an invalid expedition sends no Runner.

To sail to your own coastal city or harbour, use --to "City name" without an
explore intent. The ship docks and stays there. Everyone aboard disembarks
into that city's garrison, including all three cohorts carried by an Emporos.
No separate Unload order is needed. An empty ship can make the same journey.
Its existing home city still pays its upkeep. The harbour where the ship is
docked supplies provisions for its next voyage.

--intent land also accepts your own coastal city: it lands everyone aboard
and leaves the ship docked there. For open, unclaimed land, --q/--r is the
chosen LAND hex: the ship sails offshore, lands everyone aboard, then sails
home by itself. Add --colonize to found a colony on open ground with no
further order (--name to name it). Give the order from a ship in port.
For a foreign coastal city, use an ordinary march by a laden ship for the
existing amphibious assault; --intent land does not attack a foreign city.
A ship at sea takes no new orders — give its whole mission before departure.

Ore on mountain terrain (copper, tin, silver):
  Mountains are impassable — you cannot colonize the mountain hex itself.
  Instead, colonize an ADJACENT passable hex: the ore deposit will fall in
  the new colony's catchment and can be mined from there.
  Use 'keryx map' to see which adjacent hexes are passable.

Conquest choice (--mode, only matters when the target is an enemy settlement):
  sack (default) — loot goods (silver + a share of the rest, weighted by
    portability) and raze the settlement; the loot is carried home as a
    physical, interceptable caravan. annex — keep today's behaviour: take
    the settlement outright (a captured capital becomes an ordinary colony).

Usage:
  keryx unit march [flags]

Examples:
  keryx unit march --unit <id> --q 5 --r -3
  keryx unit march --unit <id> --target 5,-3
  keryx unit march --unit <id> --q 5 --r -3 --stance fortify
  keryx unit march --unit <id> --q 5 --r -3 --intent colonize --name Thapsos --yes
  # Colonize the hex the unit already stands on (no coords needed):
  keryx unit march --unit <id> --intent colonize --name Thapsos --yes
  # Any march reveals fog along its route toward a frontier coordinate:
  keryx unit march --unit <id> --q 12 --r -8
  # Explore an area for 12 game days, then automatically return home
  keryx unit march --unit <id> --q 12 --r -8 --intent explore --ticks 12
  # Dock in your city, disembark everyone aboard, and leave the ship there:
  keryx unit march --unit <ship-id> --to "Petras"
  # Land troops on open ground, then sail home on its own:
  keryx unit march --unit <ship-id> --q 20 --r -5 --intent land
  # ...and found a colony there with no further order:
  keryx unit march --unit <ship-id> --q 20 --r -5 --intent land --colonize --name Thapsos
  # Attack an enemy settlement and annex it instead of the sack default:
  keryx unit march --unit <id> --q 5 --r -3 --mode annex

Flags:
      --colonize        with --intent land: found a colony on arrival with no further order (use --name to name it)
      --confirm         send the order after the hiketeia warning (attacking a fallen Wanax's risen host)
  -h, --help            help for march
      --intent string   arrival intent: colonize (found a new colony — use --name to name it; omit --q/--r to colonize the hex the unit is on) | explore (first to the selected hex, then explores around it; turns home by half the chosen duration) | land (land everyone aboard in your own coastal city and stay docked, or on open ground and sail home; add --colonize only on open ground)
      --mode string     conquest choice when attacking a settlement: sack (default, loot+raze) | annex (take the city)
      --name string     colony name (with --intent colonize, or --intent land --colonize)
      --preview         estimate arrival without sending an order
      --q int           target hex Q — axial coordinate, read it off 'keryx map' (required, unless colonizing in place or using --target)
      --r int           target hex R — axial coordinate, read it off 'keryx map' (required, unless colonizing in place or using --target)
      --stance string   stance on arrival: fortify|storm|sentry
      --target string   target hex as q,r — alternative to --q/--r (e.g. 5,-3)
      --ticks int       explore duration in game days (omit for server default)
      --to string       known city name as destination — alternative to --q/--r or --target
      --unit string     exact unit name or UUID prefix (required)
      --yes             skip the colonize catchment-forecast confirmation (required for non-interactive/agent use)

Global Flags:
  -j, --json   output as JSON (for scripts and MCP)
