Keryx — Megaron's Bronze Age grand strategy CLI.
Environment: POLEIA_CONFIG selects a config file; POLEIA_SERVER and POLEIA_TOKEN override connection settings.

Usage:
  keryx [command]

Available Commands:
  abandon                  Abandon one of your colonies, freeing its hex and a settlement slot
  actions                  Show what you can do right now — and what's missing for what you can't (--province for a colony)
  agora                    Show your community chat account (outside the game)
  army                     List your forces (alias for `unit list`)
  brief                    One line per settlement you own: idle citizens, if any
  build                    Start construction of a building (--list for options, --queue to see what's queued; defaults to capital)
  build-road               Order an army to build one road edge (alias for `unit build-road`)
  call-back                Call a runner stuck waiting for passage back home, undelivered
  cancel-build             Cancel a queued building and refund costs
  cargo                    Show goods in transit to or from you (internal transfers and trade deliveries)
  cities                   List known and rumour-known settlements — city-centric (see `diplomacy` for the ruler-centric view)
  city                     Show a settlement's catchment hexes, building slots and idle pool (default: your capital)
  codex                    Read the Codex, the game's rules: no argument lists the articles, an id prints one
  completion               Generate the autocompletion script for the specified shell
  demolish                 Tear down a finished building so the hex/slot is free to rebuild (no refund; its workers become idle)
  diplomacy                List known and rumour-known Wanaxes (ruler-centric view of `cities`)
  disband                  Disband a whole unit in a city; its remaining people return there
  dispatches               Show or change which notification kinds reach you as dispatches
  dossier                  Write a markdown report of everything you know — read it first each round
  forces                   List your forces
  founding                 The Nomadic Host: your people before the metropolis (status, settle)
  god                      God-mode view — full world map and all settlements without FOW (admin only)
  goods                    Show goods inventory and production (defaults to capital; --province for a colony)
  gossip                   Show recent rumours and events
  help                     Help about any command
  hex                      Inspect known terrain and forces at their current hex
  idle                     Show how many citizens are idle (unplaced) in a settlement (default: your capital)
  inbox                    Show messenger inbox
  join                     Join the active world as a Nomadic Host — the first step before founding a city
  kingdom-borrow-army      Borrow forces from a kingdom member's settlement (disabled — post-MVP)
  kingdom-council          List a kingdom's council (disabled — post-MVP)
  kingdom-election         Show the active election for a kingdom (disabled — post-MVP)
  kingdom-election-call    Call an election for basileus (disabled — post-MVP)
  kingdom-found            Found a new kingdom (disabled — post-MVP)
  kingdom-invitations      List pending kingdom invitations (disabled — post-MVP)
  kingdom-invite           Invite a Wanax to your kingdom (disabled — post-MVP)
  kingdom-join             Join a kingdom you've been invited to (disabled — post-MVP)
  kingdom-treasury-deposit Send silver to the kingdom treasury (disabled — post-MVP)
  kingdom-vote             Cast your vote in an open basileus election (disabled — post-MVP)
  kingdoms                 List kingdoms (disabled — post-MVP)
  leave                    After your last city fell: leave this world (your account remains)
  letters                  Read letters by Wanax
  login                    Authenticate and save credentials
  map                      Show nearby visible land hexes — colony candidates and ore deposits (sorted by distance)
  march                    Order a unit to march to a hex (alias for `unit march`)
  market-board             Show or write what your city's market board says it buys and sells
  me                       Show your own realm in the selected world
  message                  Send a free-text message to another Wanax (no goods)
  messenger                Send a messenger to another settlement
  notifications            Show your notification feed (arrivals, completions, trade events, ...)
  occupation               Manage a city you currently hold under occupation
  outbox                   List sent messengers and their pending trade offers
  passage                  Arrange for a ship to carry a runner across the sea
  password                 Change your password (asks for the current one first)
  place                    Place or remove citizens on catchment hex <ordinal> doing <roll> (good)
  placements               Show a settlement's catchment hexes, building slots and idle pool (alias for `city`)
  places                   Known rivers, lakes, mountain massifs, deserts, cedar forests, islands, straits, bays, capes, plains, hills and deltas
  recall                   Recall a marching unit, or all marching forces (alias for `unit recall`)
  recruit                  Recruit a 100-man land cohort, or build a ship (--list to see all recruitable forces)
  redirect                 Redirect a marching unit to a new hex (alias for `unit redirect`)
  reinforce                Refill a thinned land cohort (alias for `unit reinforce`)
  reply                    Reply to an inbox message
  report                   Send a bug/design/confused report
  reports                  List player reports for this world (admin only)
  retreat-default          Show or set when your armies retreat by default (realm-wide)
  rich-offering            Give a temple a rich offering on top of its tithe (+50% effect while paid in full)
  rise                     After your last city fell: gather the survivors as a new host on another shore
  rite                     Perform a cultural prayer at your settlement (requires temple + offering)
  roads                    Your unfinished roads, and (--edges) the finished road edges you know of
  roster                   List every placed gubbe across all your settlements, with idle pools
  route                    Create a standing order — a caravan that keeps a destination's stock topped up on its own
  route-delete             Delete a standing order (an in-flight caravan still completes, as an ordinary transfer)
  route-pause              Pause a standing order (an in-flight caravan still completes)
  route-resume             Resume a paused standing order
  routes                   List your standing orders
  runners                  Where your runners are, and the other Wanaxes' runners you know of
  settlements              List visible settlements (use names with trade/messenger)
  sightings                List every foreign unit and garrisoned city currently in your live vision
  staff                    Show a named building workplace, or place/remove citizens with +n/-n
  status                   Show your province status (defaults to your capital; --province inspects a colony)
  storms                   Storms at sea you can see, and where you last saw the others
  ticklog                  Daily journal for a city: prod/cons, trade, builds (newest first)
  trade-accept             Accept a trade offer from inbox
  trade-cancel             Cancel an outgoing pending trade offer and reclaim escrowed silver
  trade-decline            Decline a trade offer from inbox
  trade-offer              Send a trade offer (bilateral consent, FOW-gated) to another Wanax
  transfer                 Send physical goods to your own city or a contacted foreign city as a gift, with nothing requested in return
  unit                     Manage discrete military forces
  version                  Print the commit this keryx was built from
  watch                    Listen for live push notifications over WebSocket (a wakeup call, not a ledger)
  worlds                   List available worlds

Flags:
  -h, --help   help for keryx
  -j, --json   output as JSON (for scripts and MCP)

Use "keryx [command] --help" for more information about a command.
