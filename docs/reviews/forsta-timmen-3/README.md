## 5. What worked well

- The first dispatch "Your people await you" with five numbered steps is exactly what a newcomer needs; "Review the unit" and "? Read about this" buttons on dispatches.
- Hex panels list what a hex gives (food, timber, stone, tin) before founding.
- The Buildings list says exactly what is missing ("needs 4 stone, city has 0") and when it would be ready.
- Placement UI (+1 / FILL / −1, "1 place here, 0 worked") is quick once found.
- Marching to a plain with checkbox "Explore the surrounding area, then return home" and the forecast "Arrives in 3 game days · about 6 minutes (arrives today 05:59)": clear, and the arrival report "saw 32 hexes around (42, 5): tin at (42, 3), (43, 3)" is satisfying.
- Notifications history, Search (known places and armies), Legend and Codex "How do I…?" all exist.
- The map art is beautiful; the grey remembered area after exploring is nice.

## 6. What I never found out

- Whether Itanos on a hill was a good place to found, and what I would have gained by walking elsewhere.
- What kharis does for me in practice, and which god to start with.
- How to make ships, or whether I could ever have a coast city (the founding said "no ships ever — inland").
- Whether the other two Wanaxes exist near me, how to find them, what Diplomacy's Runners do.
- What the 2nd cohort is for; how to get more soldiers (needs Barracks, 16 stone 8 timber, then Train 100).
- What "Colonize" costs.
- What the ⚒ button does.
- Whether I am slowly starving or growing: many food numbers, none labelled as "this is the one".
- How to see whether the world moves while I am away (never tested).

## 7. The one thing

Make the information the player needs to act on appear where they act. Several times the result of an action (the red refusal for the unreachable hex, the hex picker for a building, the BUILD confirmation, the unit list for the lake march) sat below the edge of the panel or not on screen at all, so I pressed things and nothing visibly happened. A newcomer reads "nothing happened" as "the game is broken", and this happened in the three most important first actions: marching, building, placing.

## 8. Would I come back tomorrow?

Probably yes, out of curiosity: the art is lovely, the world is calm, the 2-minute day makes each visit short, and the Buildings list gives me a clear next target (Barracks, a temple). What would make me quit is the feeling of silent failure: pressing March or Build and not seeing it take, the page forgetting my login on reload, and the numbers that do not update on their own. On a phone I would go on glancing at the map but I would not manage the city, because the buttons move under the finger and the text overlaps. And I still would not know why I would want a temple, an army or a second city; the hour gave me no goal other than "get more stone".

Keep working, one thing at a time, until compute runs out.
