Two notices tell you that [[potnia]] has been asked to bring down the wall of **your** city. Neither says who asked.

**Wrath gathers over the walls of your city** comes first, when someone has prayed [[the-walls-fall|The Walls Fall]] against it, but only if you stand well with [[gods|the gods]]: your Kharis must be **60 or more** ([[kharis]]). Below that you hear nothing in advance. The omen names no Wanax and no place; it is the gods warning you, not a messenger.

**The gods struck your wall** comes three game days after the prayer, always, whatever your standing. It tells you which city and the level the wall now stands at, or that it is gone. It is the same event you will see in the city view and, if a [[battle|battle]] begins, in the fight.

If you want to know who your enemies are, you must find out as a Wanax does: from messengers, from sight, from what you are told by others ([[messengers]], [[sight]]). A battle already under way at the walls keeps the wall it began with.

In Keryx these are listed by `keryx notifications`.
