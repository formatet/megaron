Swift Feet is a prayer to [[asterion]].

Runners and land [[transfers|caravans]] you name take **a quarter less time** over what is left of their road, within 12 game days of the prayer — also when they are already under way. The part already walked does not change; only what remains is shortened, and the new arrival is shown at once. A [[runners|Runner]] or caravan that leaves while the prayer holds is shortened from the start. You choose **who** is blessed; the wider the blessing, the dearer the gift.

| Who is blessed | Gift |
|---|---|
| One named Runner or caravan of yours, on the road now | the traditional offering |
| Every Runner and caravan of the city where you pray, now and while the prayer lasts | twice the traditional offering |
| Every Runner and caravan in your realm, now and while the prayer lasts | four times the traditional offering |

A journey is shortened **once**: a second prayer, or a journey that already left under a departure prayer, takes no further time off. A Runner aboard a ship follows the ship (see [[following-seas]]); its walk on land afterwards is a new journey. Only your own Runners and caravans can be named, and the same one cannot be blessed twice at once.

Offer in **Kult → Prayers** at the matching city and god: pick *One named*, *This city's* or *The whole realm*, and for one journey choose it from the list. In Keryx, `keryx rite --prayer minoan_asterion_swift --scope unit --target "<name>"`, or `--scope city` / `--scope realm`; the reply lists what arrives sooner.
