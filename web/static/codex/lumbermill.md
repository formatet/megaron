A Lumbermill improves wood production on one chosen [[catchment|catchment]] hex.

Build or upgrade it with **Choose a hex →** in **City → Buildings → Construct** (then click a highlighted hex), or with **Build here** on a hex in **City → Production**, or use `keryx build --type lumbermill` with `--hex` to choose its catchment hex.
