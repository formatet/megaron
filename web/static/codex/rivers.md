Rivers begin unnamed. The first Wanax whose own physical unit reaches a river earns the right to name it; seeing it gives no right. The river, its [[river-ford|fords]] and connected [[river-delta|delta]] share the chosen name. A named river appears in its known hex panels, map label and Keryx [[catchment|catchment]] rows. Unexplored hexes reveal no name or naming right; remembered ground keeps its name visible. See [[naming-the-land]] for the naming action and the other kinds of land.

When an expedition turns home, its dispatch says **Mission complete** and gives an estimated local clock arrival. The estimate follows the current world clock; the expedition report preserves its findings.


A known river’s channel reaches the correct edge even when its next hex is in [[sight|fog]]. Ships go with the current in half the time and against it in twice the time; [[runners|runners]], armies and [[transfers|caravans]] are not affected. A small current mark points downstream; select the hex to read the upstream/downstream directions and where it continues into fog. Keryx `map` reports the same directions. A known river also shows the kind of its connecting water hexes, including through fog: river, ford or sea. The unexplored hex’s name, resources and forces stay hidden.
