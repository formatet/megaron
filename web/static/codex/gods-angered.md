# The gods hear what you write

The [[gods|gods]] read every letter that leaves your hand and every name you give a city, a ship, a storm or a place. Coarse words do not survive the writing:

- In a **letter** or its subject, the word arrives as a scribble of signs, such as `!?@#!`.
- In a **name**, the scribe sets down an old word of the same length in its place, so the city or the mountain still has a name.

Each such word costs your realm **5 [[kharis]]**, down to its floor; standing never falls below 1. A dispatch tells you at once that the gods are angered and how much you lost. Mild oaths are not counted; slurs and crude sexual words are.

The word lists draw on the List of Dirty, Naughty, Obscene, and Otherwise Bad Words (LDNOOBW, CC BY 4.0), cut down to the coarse.
