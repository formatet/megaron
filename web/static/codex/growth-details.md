- **Variety.** Any one of the four food goods feeds people. Growth gains 10% per extra kind that forms a real share of the food eaten that game day, up to the existing variety ceiling: two substantial kinds give a 1.1 multiplier and four give 1.3. Livestock counts only if eaten as a real share of emergency food. A good merely sitting in store, or eaten only in crumbs, gives no variety bonus.

- **Size.** The city's walls set its ceiling: 2 000 people with no wall, then 5 000, 10 000 and 20 000 for a Palisade, Stone Wall and Bronze Wall ([[buildings]]). Growth runs at its full rate right up to the ceiling and then stops; a city at its ceiling never shrinks because of it. The city screen says "Walls hold N", and `keryx status` says it too.
