Livestock is the herd kept by a city. Each animal is worth **1 food**. Animals above the pen's cap are eaten first at the game day's meal, and a herd standing at its cap adds its overflow to the city's food net, so a city with a full pen grows faster than one with only [[grain|grain]], [[fish|fish]] and [[oil|oil]]. Animals under the cap are eaten only as emergency food. See [[food]] and [[pen]].

Use **Economy → Goods** or `keryx goods` to inspect actual stocks and flow. Send available cargo through Transfer or `keryx transfer --good livestock --qty AMOUNT --dest CITY`.
