Megaron is being built while you play it. When something is wrong, the most useful thing you can do is tell us — from inside the game.

Open **Report (✎)** and choose:

- **Bug — something is broken**,
- **Design — this feels wrong**, or
- **Confused — I don't understand this**.

Write what happened and press **Send**. Your position on the map, the current game day and what you were looking at are attached automatically, so you do not need to describe them.

A *Confused* report is as valuable as a bug. If this Codex did not answer your question, that is worth a report too.

Before reporting, glance at [[rough-edges]] — it may already be known.

A dispatch action names the surface it opens: **Staff** a completed workplace, **Show** its city, **Review** a unit, **Reply** to a correspondent or **Manage route**. The selected city, site, unit or route is marked on that surface. Cargo already delivered leads to the destination city's goods; a cargo still travelling leads to its transit row. An old dispatch without a remaining target has no navigation button.
