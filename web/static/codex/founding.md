Founding your first city — the **metropolis** — is the one decision in Megaron you cannot undo. The Host dissolves forever.

## Read the ground first

While the [[horde|Host]] panel is open it shows a **founding forecast** for the hex you stand on, and it updates as you walk. The [[catchment]] you would claim glows on the map.

The forecast gives the grain your fields would produce against what your people would eat, and one of three verdicts:

- **It feeds itself** — the fields cover the mouths with room to spare.
- **Marginal** — positive but thin. Draft one cohort and you are eating into the granary.
- **It does not feed itself** — you are told how many days your carried grain buys, and whether a Farm would fix it. If it says a farm will not help, there is no farmable ground in reach at all.

The gap between *marginal* and *does not feed itself* has killed more young cities than any war. See [[food]].

Below the grain line, look for **deposits**: copper, tin, silver, cedar. A site with metal under it is worth walking extra days for. A site with none is not a mistake — you can buy metal ([[trade]]) — but you will be buying it from someone who walked those days. Read [[bronze]] before you decide.

## Where you cannot found

Not on sea, mountain, river or semi-desert, and **your catchment may not overlap another settlement's**. The game refuses and tells you how far to move.

## Settling

Press **⚒ Found the metropolis here** at the bottom of the Host panel, and confirm. In exchange:

- The Host's people become your city's population.
- Everything carried — grain and silver — pours into the city.
- Units standing on the hex become its garrison.
- **Demeter's gift**: if there is farmable ground in your catchment, a Farm appears, free.
- **Poseidon's gift**: found on the coast and a galley is waiting in the harbour.
- Your first [[citizens]] are placed on the best food land automatically, until the city feeds itself. The rest stand idle, waiting for you.
