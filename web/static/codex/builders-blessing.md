The Builder's Blessing is a prayer to [[velchanos]].

What is **left** of every building and every ship under construction in the blessed city takes **three quarters of the time**, and it is shown at once in the city's construction list. Each build is shortened **once**, however many prayers are said; a second blessing takes no further time off. A build that **begins** while the blessing lasts (12 game days with the traditional gift; a larger gift lasts longer) is shortened from its start. Builds waiting in the queue are not touched until they begin. A repair is not construction and is not shortened, and no build ever finishes sooner than the next game day.

| Who is blessed | Gift |
|---|---|
| The city where you pray | twice the traditional offering |
| Every city of your realm | four times the traditional offering |

The same city cannot be blessed twice at once. Only your own cities are reached.

Offer in **Kult → Prayers** at a [[temple]] of Velchanos. In Keryx, `keryx rite --prayer minoan_velchanos_building --scope city` or `--scope realm`; the reply lists each build that is ready sooner.
