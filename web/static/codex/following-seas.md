Following Seas is a prayer to the [[sea-daimons|Sea Daimons]] of Stillness.

Ships you name take **a quarter less time** over what is left of their voyage, within 12 game days of the prayer — also when they are already at sea. The part already sailed does not change; only what remains is shortened, and the new arrival is shown at once. This holds for a ship marching and for a ship carrying freight. A ship that sails while the prayer holds is shortened from the start. You choose **who** is blessed; the wider the blessing, the dearer the gift.

| Who is blessed | Gift |
|---|---|
| One named ship of yours | the traditional offering |
| Every ship supported by the city where you pray | twice the traditional offering |
| Every ship in your realm | four times the traditional offering |

A voyage is shortened **once**: a second prayer, or a ship that already left under a departure prayer, takes no further time off. A ship on an expedition keeps its schedule. A [[runners|Runner]] aboard travels at the ship's actual speed. Only your own ships can be named, and the same ship cannot be blessed twice at once. [[storms]] are another matter: see [[stout-hulls]].

Offer in **Kult → Prayers** at the matching city and god: pick *One named*, *This city's* or *The whole realm*, and for one ship choose it from the list. In Keryx, `keryx rite --prayer minoan_stillness_swift --scope unit --target "<name>"`, or `--scope city` / `--scope realm`; the reply lists what arrives sooner.
