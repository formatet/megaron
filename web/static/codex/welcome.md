You are a **Wanax**: a Bronze Age ruler of a people on the eastern shores of a great sea. Many of you share one world, and it keeps moving whether you are watching or not.

This Codex is the world's book of knowledge. Every article links onward — follow what you are curious about, or open it from the **?** on any drawer or notification to read about exactly what is in front of you.

## Three rules that explain almost everything

**A day is a tick.** The world advances one day at a time, and every duration the game shows you counts in game-days. See [[time]].

**The world runs without you.** Nobody pauses. Fields are worked, soldiers are paid and granaries fill and empty while you sleep. You do not win by being online more; you win by leaving good [[routes|standing orders]] and by reading [[coming-back|what happened while you were gone]].

**Command is never instant.** An order to an army in the field travels by Runner, a person who has to walk there. Until he arrives, the army does what it was last told. See [[runners]].
