Favourable Passage is a prayer to The [[sea-daimons|Sea Daimons]] of Stillness.

The next ship leaving this port sails faster and takes no [[storms|storm]] damage on its outward leg.

| Reference | Current rule |
|---|---|
| God | The Sea Daimons of Stillness |
| Minimum standing | 5 Kharis |
| Traditional offering | 6 fish · 3 silver |
| Temple’s rest (level 1, traditional gift) | 12 game days |

Offer in **Kult → Prayers** at the matching city and god, or use `keryx rite --prayer minoan_stillness_passage`. A valid composed offering can replace the traditional recipe.
