Favourable Passage is a prayer to The Sea Daimons of Stillness.

The next ship leaving this port sails faster and takes no storm damage on its outward leg.

<!-- facts: prayer minoan_stillness_passage -->
| Reference | Current rule |
|---|---|
| God | The Sea Daimons of Stillness |
| Minimum standing | 5 Kharis |
| Traditional offering | 3 silver · 6 fish |
| God’s waiting time | 12 game days |
<!-- /facts -->

Offer in **Kult → Prayers** at the matching city and god, or use `keryx rite --prayer minoan_stillness_passage`. A valid composed offering can replace the traditional recipe.
