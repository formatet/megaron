- **♫** silences all sound: the sign-in screen's opening theme, the music, the horn when troops receive a march order, the clash of a resolved battle, a short call when your forces arrive, and a soft chime for each new dispatch while you are playing.

- **⚒** toggles an activity overlay.

On mobile, windows fill the screen. Use the close button at the top right to return to the map. Opening another window closes the previous one.
