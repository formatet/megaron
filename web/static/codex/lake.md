A lake is still water inland, cut off from the [[sea|Thalassa]]; its fish feed the cities on its shore.

A lake is water the open sea does not reach. Many lakes still have a [[rivers|river]] that runs out of them to the sea, and a ship can sail that way. Land forces cannot cross a lake. From its shore you see across it as far as across the sea ([[sight]]). **No [[storms|storm]] ever forms on a lake or drifts onto one.**

A city beside a lake counts as coastal: it may build a [[harbour]]. A [[shipyard]] needs a waterway to the Thalassa — lake, river or [[river-ford|ford]] all the way to the sea. On a lake with no such way out, the shipyard is refused: *this lake has no waterway to the Thalassa*.

**[[galley|Galley]] base speed: 3 hexes per game day**, as on coastal water. See [[ship-types]].

## Base production

A lake fishes like [[coastal-sea|coastal water]]. It is never a bay, however much land surrounds it, and a harbour adds nothing to it. Each worker is one [[citizens|citizen]] group.
