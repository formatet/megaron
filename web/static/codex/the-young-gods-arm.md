The Young God's Arm is a prayer to [[velchanos]].

Land troops you name fight a quarter stronger for 6 game days, wherever they stand: attacking or defending, in the field or in a city. You choose **who** is blessed; the wider the blessing, the dearer the gift.

| Who is blessed | Gift |
|---|---|
| One named unit of yours | the traditional offering |
| Every unit supported by the city where you pray | twice the traditional offering |
| Every land unit in your realm | four times the traditional offering |

The blessing is read when it is needed, not when a unit marches: it helps a unit already on the road, and a prayer said in the middle of a [[battle|battle]] works from the next battle round. Troops raised after the prayer are covered too if they belong to the blessed city or realm. Only your own troops can be named. It does not strengthen ships. The same troops cannot be blessed twice at once; a larger gift lasts longer, as with other lasting prayers, measured against the gift for the step you chose.

Offer in **Kult → Prayers** at the matching city and god: pick *One named*, *This city's* or *The whole realm*, and for one unit choose it from the list. In Keryx, `keryx rite --prayer minoan_velchanos_strength --scope unit --target "<name>"`, or `--scope city` / `--scope realm`; `keryx rite --list` shows the troops you can name and the gift for each step.
