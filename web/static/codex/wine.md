Wine is not food: it is the drink of [[gods|the gods]] and the feast, offered in [[rites|prayers]]. Vines grow only on **[[hills|hills]]**, so a city without hills in reach must trade for it. A [[winery]] refines more wine from the city's hill vines.

Use **Economy → Goods** or `keryx goods` to inspect actual stocks and flow. Send available cargo through Transfer or `keryx transfer --good wine --qty AMOUNT --dest CITY`.

See [[goods]], [[citizens]] and [[trade]].
