**Rumour is not contact.** [[gossip|Gossip]] tells you that a city exists and roughly where, deliberately blurred, but it does not open the door. Seeing a city on the map ([[sight]]) does not either.

Cities you have contacted appear in **Diplomacy → Letters → Write**. Choose **Write** to ask their Wanax what they need or can spare; market knowledge comes through [[messengers|correspondence]].

Contact reveals that city’s location, not the surrounding land or its current activity.

**A letter you receive tells you who wrote, not where they live.** It opens no contact and puts nothing on your map. You can still answer it — your reply goes home with the same runner while it waits.

Opening a letter in an exchange marks it as read. The Diplomacy badge counts unread letters; an unanswered offer can remain pending after you have read it. `keryx inbox` marks the letters it displays as read too. Reading does not send the messenger home or accept a trade.
