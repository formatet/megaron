The world advances one **game day** at a time. Wherever the game tells you how long something takes — a march, a building, a letter — it counts in game days, because that is the unit your decisions are made in.

How much real time a game day takes is set per world. A test world can run faster than normal. The **date in the top bar** is the world's own calendar; the sun and moon beside it show the time in the world. **When something happens** uses your clock and date: **today 18:34**, **tomorrow 09:10**, or **Thu 09:00**; further away, a date is shown. **How long something lasts** stays in game days of the world: **food for 12 game days**, or **a 30-game-day expedition**. Arrival estimates follow the world's current pace; they can change when the world pauses or its pace changes.
