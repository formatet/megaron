Any good can be offered, and wine and oil are offerings too ([[rites]]) — wine is also food, so a hungry city should keep it. For everyday prayers, incense can help meet the offering requirement, but more offering never improves the answer. For older prayers with uncertain answers, **a gift made mostly of incense can please the gods further than any other gift**: it raises the limit on what a larger offering can achieve. It never replaces your standing with the gods ([[kharis]]) — a god who does not hear you is not bought — but it is the best use of what you carry. Each god's taste is shown in the Offer row and in `keryx rite --list`; the gods of sight and omens love it most.
