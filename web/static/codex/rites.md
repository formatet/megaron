A **rite** is a request to the gods. Open **Kult → Prayers**.

Each prayer names its god, the minimum standing it needs, its traditional offering, and what that god particularly favours. Press **Perform →**.

Or expand **Compose your own offering** and build it yourself: set quantities from your stores and watch the live estimate — *"estimated worth X of ~Y expected"* — then **Offer this →**. A richer offering improves your odds; a stingy one hurts them.

## A rite is a wager

The offering is consumed whether the gods answer or not, and the rite **spends kharis** either way. Its chance is essentially your standing ([[kharis]]). You are never shown the exact odds — only the gods' mood, and whether they seem worth asking. That is on purpose.

After a rite, the prayer cannot be repeated for a while.

## The prayers

- **The oracle** — reveals ore deposits in the region. The best tool in the game for finding metal you cannot see ([[bronze]], [[sight]]).
- **The harvest blessing** — swells your grain.
- **Battle frenzy** — strengthens a garrison for a while ([[battle]]).
