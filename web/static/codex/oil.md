Oil is a food and offering good. Most of it comes from **olive groves**; plains and hills give only a third as much per worker, as timber does outside the forest. An Olive Press refines more.

Use **Economy → Goods** or `keryx goods` to inspect actual stocks and flow. Send available cargo through Transfer or `keryx transfer --good oil --qty AMOUNT --dest CITY`.
