Oil is a food and offering good. Most of it comes from **[[olive-grove|olive groves]]**; [[plains|plains]] and [[hills|hills]] give only a third as much per worker, as [[timber|timber]] does outside the forest. An [[olive-press]] refines more.

Use **Economy → Goods** or `keryx goods` to inspect actual stocks and flow. Send available cargo through Transfer or `keryx transfer --good oil --qty AMOUNT --dest CITY`.

See [[goods]], [[citizens]] and [[trade]].
