Winning at a city — by [[battle]] or by [[sieges|siege]] — does not transfer it. It becomes **occupied**: still owned by its Wanax, garrisoned by the victor's survivors, its production frozen.

This is a deliberately contestable window. **Any new fighting resets the clock.** Both sides are told so plainly; the defender learns that the city is *not lost yet — a relief force resets the enemy's countdown.*
