Winning at a city — by [[battle]] or by [[sieges|siege]] — does not transfer it. It becomes **occupied**: still owned by its Wanax, garrisoned by the victor's survivors, its production frozen.

This is a deliberately contestable window. **Any new fighting resets the clock.** Both sides are told so plainly; the defender learns that the city is *not lost yet — a relief force resets the enemy's countdown.*

## When the occupation ripens

Hold it unchallenged long enough and you are told the city is ripe. Then you may:

- **sack** it — loot it and weaken it; it stays theirs;
- **burn** it — sack it, then raze it; nobody may resettle there for a long while;
- **annex** it — keep it as your own.

Doing nothing leaves it occupied, which is the safe and reversible choice.

**These three choices cannot yet be made in the browser** — only from the command line with `keryx occupation` ([[keryx]], [[rough-edges]]).

## When it happens to you

You are notified when your city is occupied, when an occupation is broken, and — if it comes to that — when it is sacked, looted, burned or taken. A relief force is your answer: every fight at the city resets the occupier's countdown.
