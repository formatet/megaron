A unit that runs out of food **starves**: it loses men each game day, and can starve to nothing. A ship turns home when its remaining food is only enough for the way back, unless it can reach a city for provisions. Its owner hears the reason when it reaches home, not from far away; see [[ship-food-return]].

The Notifications archive marks when a city’s garrison starts losing men to hunger and when it stops. A recovery means that loss has stopped; it does not promise food for every soldier who may arrive later. Produce more food or send food by [[transfers|transfer]].
