When your last city falls, to hunger, to a conqueror's annexation or to fire, your reign in it is over. The screen that follows tells the story of that reign, from the city's rise to its fall. Under the story you choose what comes next.

## Gather the survivors

Survivors set out again as a small [[horde|Nomadic Host]]:

- **Between 400 and 800 people.** The number falls as it falls. <!-- src: api/handlers/rise.go risenHostMinGubbar/risenHostMaxGubbar -->
- **One company of spearmen** as escort, where a newcomer has two.
- **60 silver**, a fixed reserve; the spearman escort draws no silver pay. Bread for the road is the same as a newcomer carries.
- **Another shore.** Refugees do not stay where the enemy stands. The Host is set down on another landmass, or on an island, wherever there is free ground.

Until you found a city, your Host stands under the gods' protection: anyone who attacks it loses 70 kharis. See [[xenia]].

Everything you knew stays yours: your letters, the cities you have [[contact|made contact with]] and the map you have seen. Then it is [[founding]] again, as at the very start.

## Leave this world

You leave this world for good and cannot rise in it again. **Your account remains**, and the next world is open to you. To delete the account itself, write to the admin in the [[community-chat|community chat]].

In [[keryx|Keryx]] the same two choices are `keryx rise` and `keryx leave`.

See also: [[occupation]] and [[sieges]], for how a city is lost, and [[xenia]] for the gods' order among the Wanaxes.
