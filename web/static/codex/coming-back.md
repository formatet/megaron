While you were away, the world kept moving. Here is how to catch up.

## Open the Notifications archive (☍)

It is a permanent record of everything that happened to you — there is deliberately no "clear all". Urgent events are marked. Critical food warnings and a garrison’s latest hunger warning appear first. When that city’s recovery arrives, its old hunger warning returns to its place in history. Each notification shows when it happened using your clock and date, such as **yesterday 14:05**. If its time cannot be established, it says **time unavailable**.

Click any row to open it in the **Dispatch window**. The same window opens from a dispatch chip in the top bar. It gives you:

- what happened,
- **⌖ Take me there**, which jumps the map to where it happened,
- on a letter or a trade offer, **✉ Reply** instead, which opens your letters with that Wanax (in Keryx: `keryx letters <id>`, printed under the notice),
- **? Read about this**, which opens the Codex article that explains it,
- a checkbox, *Stop these as dispatches*. Muting only silences the chip. The event is always kept in the archive. In [[keryx|Keryx]] the same setting is `keryx dispatches --mute <Kind>`, and it is shared between the two.

## What to act on first

1. **Food** — a [[food|shortfall]] kills people every game day it lasts.
2. **Pay and bread** — [[upkeep]] warnings mean soldiers need silver or food. Soldiers share grain, fish, oil and wine with the people, but their meals are itemized separately. Produce more food or send a [[transfers|transfer]]. A notice marks when garrison hunger starts and when it ends.
3. **Foreign marches** — someone is coming, and you know roughly when. See [[defence]].
4. **Sieges and occupation** — a clock is running on a city. See [[sieges]] and [[occupation]].

A [[kharis|Kharis]] notice marks each change between Favorable, Indifferent, Suspicious and Wrathful. Check **Kult** for Kharis and the upkeep of the cult. It fades from the start; every active city and its residents count toward the cost. A warning marks a fall below 30 with negative net; below 15 punishment can fall, with or without a temple. An actual punishment protects you for seven game days; Kult shows when this protection ends. Staff and feed temples, then check the projected net.

Then look at **City → Production** for idle [[citizens]], and **Diplomacy → Correspondence** to ask a contacted Wanax about [[trade]].

## Making the next absence easier

Set up [[routes|standing orders]] for food and materials, keep [[food|food]] in the city’s stores, and post a [[stances|watch]] where enemies would come from.

Historical food reserve releases, food support reports and food reserve warnings are grouped at the end of Notifications. Choose **show reports** to read them; **All notifications** returns to the archive. Rumours you have heard are mixed into the same list, each marked **Rumour**.
