Wake the Storm is a prayer to the [[sea-daimons|Sea Daimons]] of Stillness.

A new [[storms|storm]] is born on a **sea hex of your own map**: the hex you name and two sea hexes beside it where no other storm stands. If there is no room, the prayer is refused and your gift is not taken. The new storm lives as long as any storm (some 200 to 300 game days), has no name until the first Wanax whose ship it strikes names it, and **strikes every ship it meets, yours too**.

No one is told that a storm was woken. It looks like any storm that was born on the [[sea|Thalassa]]. You know only the hex you named, and a Wanax who sees the storm sees what anyone sees.

You can only wake a storm where you can already see or remember the water. A richer gift does no more: the prayer is fixed.

Offer in **Kult → Prayers** at the matching city and god, and enter the hex as `q,r` (or click a sea hex on the map first). In Keryx, `keryx rite --prayer minoan_stillness_wake --hex q,r`.
