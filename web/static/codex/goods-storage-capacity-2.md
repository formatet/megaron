A **StorageOverflow** notification means goods have already been lost because storage was full. It records the city, good, quantity lost and the source, such as production, a delivery, returned provisions or a refund. It does not mean the entire shipment was lost: only the reported excess failed to fit. Production into a store that stays full is lost every game day; you are told when it starts, not every game day it goes on. If the forecast clears and the city later approaches capacity again, a new warning can be sent.
