If you cannot pay, you are warned first; if it goes on, **men desert**, and a unit that loses everyone is gone. Recruit professionals only when you can keep paying them. A spearman cohort costs 12 grain and 100 people to recruit, with no silver.

An empty ship **Laid up** in its own Harbour costs **0 silver and 0 ship food**. Its crew has returned to the city’s population and eats as civilians. **Fit out** drafts that crew back immediately; home-rate ship food and, for a War Galley, half field pay resume while the ship spends three game days preparing.
