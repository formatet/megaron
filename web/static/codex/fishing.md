Fish feeds your people just like grain ([[food]]). Place [[citizens]] on a fishing hex in **City**, or use `keryx place fish <hex> +1`. The available places and the next citizen’s output come from that city’s current production options.

| Water | Places without a harbour | Places with a harbour |
|---|---|---|
| Coastal water | 2 | 6 |
| Bay | 3 | 7 |
| Deep water | 0 | 4 |

A **bay** is coastal water with land on at least four of its six sides. Rivers and fords count as land for this rule; coastal water, deep water and the Thalassa do not. A missing hex beyond the map edge does not count as land. The extra place belongs to the bay itself.

Deep-water fishing requires the city’s [[harbour]]. The harbour also raises each fisher’s production on coastal and deep water. Rivers and fords keep their own fishing places and receive no harbour bonus.

A harbour creates places, not workers. Put citizens on those water hexes to use them; raising the harbour’s level increases output per worker rather than adding more places.
