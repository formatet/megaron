Food storage is limited (see [[goods]] and [[granary]]); other goods can pile up to a million. Either way, **surplus is not free.**

The **Economy → Goods** tab marks goods that are piling up with nothing using them. The waste is not shelf space. It is the [[citizens]] standing on that hex, producing something nobody needs, when they could be growing food, cutting [[timber|timber]] or mining metal.

The fix is always the same: **move them.** Take them off the hex that overproduces and put them where the *next citizen would add* the most that you actually use. Or sell the surplus to someone who wants it ([[trade]] — ask a contacted Wanax what they need in a [[messengers|letter]]).

This is the most valuable habit in Megaron, and the one most players never build.
