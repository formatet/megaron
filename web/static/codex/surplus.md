Storage is effectively unlimited, but **surplus is not free.**

The **Economy → Goods** tab marks goods that are piling up with nothing using them. The waste is not shelf space. It is the [[citizens]] standing on that hex, producing something nobody needs, when they could be growing food, cutting timber or mining metal.

The fix is always the same: **move them.** Take them off the hex that overproduces and put them where the *next citizen would add* the most that you actually use. Or sell the surplus to someone who wants it ([[trade]] — the **Wants** tab shows who).

This is the most valuable habit in Megaron, and the one most players never build.
