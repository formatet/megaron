**Kharis** is your standing with the gods — a relationship, not a resource. It is held for your whole realm, not per city, and your [[account|Account]] summary shows its current amount. The **Kult (⛩)** drawer shows it as a mood:

| Mood | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Favorable** | The gods may bless you: a blessed harvest (a quarter more food for 25 days, fish excepted) or unexpected recruits. |
| **Indifferent** | They have noticed you and do nothing. |
| **Suspicious** | You are exposed to punishment. |
| **Wrathful** | Chariots break, ships are lost, granaries rot, plague takes the garrison. |

Blessings and punishments arrive as notifications.

Your Host begins at the Kharis floor, even before founding. Rising again begins there too. Kharis never falls all the way to nothing, and a newly founded Wanax is protected from punishment for the first 60 days. Build a temple before they run out.

## How to raise it

Three things must be true, every day:

1. **You have a temple.** Without one, kharis decays.
2. **People serve in it** — devotion. See [[temples]].
3. **The temple is fed** — a small daily offering of oil and wine.

**Your best temple sets your ceiling.** A small temple keeps you out of trouble; it can never earn you a blessing. If you want the gods on your side, build them something grand.

Kharis decays every day regardless — and faster for each colony without a temple, beyond a handful. Expansion has a religious price ([[colonies]]).

To *spend* kharis on a request, see [[rites]].

An ongoing **blessed harvest** appears beside Food in the city drawer and in
`keryx status` and `keryx city`, with its yield bonus, affected goods and days left.
The row disappears when the blessing ends; fish receives no harvest bonus.
