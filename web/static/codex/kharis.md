**Kharis** is your standing with the gods — a relationship, not a resource. It is held for your whole realm, not per city, and your [[account|Account]] summary shows its current amount. The **Kult (⛩)** drawer shows it as a mood:

| Mood | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Favorable** | The gods may bless you: a blessed harvest (a quarter more food for 25 days, fish excepted) or unexpected recruits. |
| **Indifferent** | They have noticed you and do nothing. |
| **Suspicious** | You are exposed to punishment. |
| **Wrathful** | Chariots break, ships are lost, granaries rot, plague takes the garrison. |

Blessings and punishments arrive as notifications.

Your first Host begins at the Kharis floor, even before founding. Rising again keeps your standing with the gods, with the floor as a minimum. Kharis never falls all the way to nothing, and a newly founded Wanax is protected from punishment for the first 60 days. Build a temple before they run out.

## How to raise it

Three things must be true, every day:

1. **You have a temple.** Without one, kharis decays.
2. **People serve in it** — devotion. See [[temples]].
3. **The temple is fed** — a daily reference gift of 0.25 grain, with local fish, timber or silver as substitutes of equal divine value. See [[temples]] for the selection order and planned gift.

A fully staffed, fed level-1 temple lets your standing grow slowly from the floor toward early [[rites|prayers]]. Larger temples can employ more devotion and raise kharis faster when staffed and fed.

**Your best temple sets your ceiling.** A level-1 temple can never earn you a blessing. To reach blessings, you need a higher-level temple.

Kharis decays every day regardless — and faster for each colony without a temple, beyond a handful. Expansion has a religious price ([[colonies]]).

To *spend* kharis on a request, see [[rites]].

An ongoing **blessed harvest** appears beside Food in the city drawer and in
`keryx status` and `keryx city`, with its yield bonus, affected goods and days left.
The row disappears when the blessing ends; fish receives no harvest bonus.

The gods reckon the temple's offering against the same workday reference as [[rites|prayers]], including the labour behind each good. World scarcity can raise what devotion earns; it is measured against that reference rather than a buying price. The ordinary daily offering and your choice of devotion remain separate from a prayer's one-time gift.
