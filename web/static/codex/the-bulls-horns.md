The Bull's Horns is a prayer to [[asterion]].

Land troops you name strike **half as hard again in the first round of a [[battle]]**, for 12 game days: attacking or defending, in the field or in a city. After that first round they fight as usual. You choose **who** is blessed; the wider the blessing, the dearer the gift.

| Who is blessed | Gift |
|---|---|
| One named unit of yours | the traditional offering |
| Every unit supported by the city where you pray | twice the traditional offering |
| Every land unit in your realm | four times the traditional offering |

The horns count only at the opening of a battle: round one of the battle's opening. A troop that joins a battle once it has begun, or a battle already past its first round when you pray, gets nothing from them. They stack with [[the-young-gods-arm]]: in that first round the two multiply. The blessing is read when it is needed, wherever the troops stand, so it helps a unit already on the road; troops raised after the prayer are covered too if they belong to the blessed city or realm. Only your own troops can be named, and it does not help ships. The same troops cannot be blessed twice at once. A larger gift lasts longer, as with other lasting prayers, measured against the gift for the step you chose. There is no step for ever.

Your force list says "the bull's horns" with the game days left. Others see only the result of the battle.

Offer in **Kult → Prayers** at the matching city and god: pick *One named*, *This city's* or *The whole realm*, and for one unit choose it from the list. In Keryx, `keryx rite --prayer minoan_asterion_horns --scope unit --target "<name>"`, or `--scope city` / `--scope realm`; `keryx rite --list` shows the troops you can name and the gift for each step.
