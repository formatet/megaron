You do not need to break a wall. Put forces in **sentry** ([[stances]]) on the hexes a city works, and those hexes stop feeding it. Hold that long enough, with the city's food need uncovered, and **it capitulates on its own** — without a battle. The wall is never breached; the belly gives up.

This is the most powerful thing in the game that needs no [[bronze]], and the reason a city that cannot feed itself from its own [[catchment]] is fragile however thick its walls are.
