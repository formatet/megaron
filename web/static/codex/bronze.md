This is the strategic shape of the whole world.

Bronze needs **copper and tin**. **Copper and tin are never found on the same landmass** — the world is made that way on purpose, every time. It is not bad luck, and no new world will fix it.

Therefore **nobody can make bronze alone.** Not the richest Wanax, not the luckiest start. Bronze — and with it elite infantry, war chariots, war galleys and the best walls — exists only for those who cross water, or who trade with someone who has.

## Three roads

1. **Mine one half and buy the other.** The merchant's road, and the most common ([[trade]]).
2. **Sail and found a colony on the other metal's landmass.** Expensive, slow, permanent ([[sea]], [[colonies]]).
3. **Take it from someone carrying it.** A caravan of tin crossing your ground is a target ([[stances]]).

Then smelt it: a **Foundry** turns copper and tin into bronze ([[buildings]]).

## If you cannot find copper at all

Go and look — that is what the world is for. Every ship and every scout widens the map ([[sight]]). And you can ask the **oracle** ([[rites]]): it reveals a deposit somewhere you have never been. Getting there is up to you.
