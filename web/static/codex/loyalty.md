**Loyalty** is a settlement's willingness to be yours. It is a small scale, not a percentage, and it changes for reasons you can see.

The **City → Production → Loyalty log** lists every change to a settlement's loyalty and why.

It matters concretely in [[battle]]: loyal troops commit more of their men each round and rout later; wavering ones hold back.

Loyalty is mostly a question for [[colonies]], far from the capital and newly won.

A [[transfers|gift]] from your capital is the direct lever: 50 or more silver-equivalent earns +1.
