**Silver is the currency and pays your professional forces.** Bronze Guard, chariots and War Galleys need it; spearmen, Galleys and Emporoi need no silver to recruit or maintain.

Professionals draw pay every game day. A Wanax who runs dry risks their desertion — see [[upkeep]].

## Where silver comes from

- **Out of the ground.** A silver deposit in your [[catchment]] and a **Mine** built on that deposit hex ([[buildings]]) — the same building that works a copper or tin hex; the hex decides what it produces.
- **Out of someone else's ground, through [[trade]].** Most Wanaxes will not have a deposit. That is the point: silver is why the map has merchants on it.

Silver is kept with your other goods — the **Economy → Goods** tab shows your balance ([[goods]]).

Founding moves only the silver your Host still carries into the city: a new Host begins with **120 silver**, inland or coastal. Founding adds no extra silver. A Host rising after losing every city carries its remaining refugee purse. A colony carries existing silver from its mother city, rather than receiving new currency.
