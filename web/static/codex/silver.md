**Silver is the currency and pays your professional forces.** Elite infantry, chariots and ships need it; spearmen live on grain and draw no silver pay.

Professionals draw pay every day. A Wanax who runs dry risks their desertion — see [[upkeep]].

## Where silver comes from

- **Out of the ground.** A silver deposit in your [[catchment]] and a **Mine** built on that deposit hex ([[buildings]]) — the same building that works a copper or tin hex; the hex decides what it produces.
- **Out of someone else's ground, through [[trade]].** Most Wanaxes will not have a deposit. That is the point: silver is why the map has merchants on it.

Silver is kept with your other goods — the **Economy → Goods** tab shows your balance ([[goods]]).

Your first founding fills the treasury to at least **730 silver inland**, or **1,278 on the coast**, including what your Host still carries. This fixed reserve pays for recruitment, professional forces, trade and offerings; spearmen do not draw daily silver. A Host rising after losing every city carries only its remaining refugee purse; founding again adds no new endowment. A colony carries existing silver from its mother city, rather than receiving new currency.
