The gods are real in Megaron. Their goodwill toward your people is [[kharis]]: kept up by temple workers and daily offerings, lost through neglect. In return they answer prayers ([[rites]]) and send true omens ([[gossip]]).

Each god has their own [[temple|Temple]]. A temple opens that god's prayers in its city; build it again to raise its level, which raises how much kharis your realm can hold ([[temples]]).

The gods do not want to be challenged. A ruler who neglects them meets their wrath ([[kharis]]).
