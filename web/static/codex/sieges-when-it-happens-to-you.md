You get a notification when a siege begins and when it is lifted. The City drawer carries a red banner naming who holds the approaches, and the map draws a dark cloud over the city.

What helps:

- full [[food|city food stores]] — the city eats its grain, fish and oil while the fields are cut off;
- food brought in from outside by [[transfers|transfer]] — if it can get through;
- a relief force that drives the besiegers off the hexes ([[battle]]).

If the city falls, see [[occupation]].
