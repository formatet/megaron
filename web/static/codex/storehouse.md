A Storehouse holds everything a city keeps except food, the herd and cult.

Build or upgrade it in **City → Buildings → Construct**, or use `keryx build --type storehouse`.

## How the shared store fills {#storehouse-fills}

The City drawer's Warehouse shows one line, **Stores X / Y**: everything in the shared store against its ceiling. Goods in it have no cap of their own. A good counts against the store as soon as it is held, so a city rich in stone has less room for timber.

While there is room, every good grows at its own rate. When the store fills, **all production stops together**: the last room is shared out in proportion to the rates, so no good goes first. Two goods produced at the same rate with 10 places left each get 5. Deliveries (trade, gifts, [[transfers|transfers]], refunds) fill whatever room is left; the part that does not fit is lost, and the notice says how much.

You get **one dispatch** when production starts to be lost, not one per good and not one every game day. It is told again only after 24 game days in which nothing was lost. Spend goods, send them away, or build or upgrade a Storehouse to make room.

A city that already holds more than its ceiling keeps everything: nothing is ever taken away, new goods are simply not admitted until use brings the total under the ceiling. Demolishing a Storehouse lowers the ceiling the same way.
