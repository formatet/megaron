A fed city grows a little every day. Three things govern how much:

- **Grain in store**, not grain production. A full granary grows; a city living hand to mouth does not, even with a positive balance.
- **Variety.** Grain alone feeds people. Fish, oil, wine and livestock alongside it make them multiply faster.
- **Size.** Growth slows as a city approaches its practical ceiling.

A city always holds back a small grain reserve that growth may never touch. Dipping below it stops growth for the day but starves nobody — so a city cannot grow itself into a famine.

Each time the population crosses another hundred, a new [[citizens|citizen]] appears and places itself on your best food hex.

More people means more workers, more recruits and more mouths. See [[food]].
