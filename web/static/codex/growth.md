A city grows when it has a **surplus of food** — when it produces more food each game day than its people eat. Growing costs no food: the surplus stays in the same city grain, fish and oil stocks that feed your people.

- **Surplus, not store.** What counts is the daily balance — grain, fish and oil produced, minus what the people eat — shown as the food *net* on the city. A positive net grows the city; zero or below holds it steady, even with full stores.

- **Surplus size.** A bigger surplus fills your stores faster; it does not itself raise the growth rate.
