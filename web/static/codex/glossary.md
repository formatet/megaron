**Catchment** — the land a settlement works: its own hex and the ring within two steps. See [[catchment]].

**Citizen** — one marker on the placement grid; one hundred people. See [[citizens]].

**Contact** — the permanent right to write to and trade with a city, earned by reaching it. See [[contact]].

**Emporos** — a merchant ship. See [[sea]].

**Kharis** — your standing with the gods. See [[kharis]].

**Kult** — the drawer for the gods and the temple.

**Metropolis** — your first city, founded from the Nomadic Host. See [[founding]].

**Nomadic Host** — the people you begin as, before you found a city. See [[horde]].

**Runner** — the person who carries your order to a unit in the field. See [[runners]].

**Sentry** — the watch stance; also how a siege is laid. See [[stances]].

**Sitos** — a city's public granary. See [[sitos]].

**Standing order** — a caravan route that runs by itself. See [[routes]].

**The Thalassa** — the sea. See [[sea]].

**Tick** — one day in the world. See [[time]].

**Wanax** — a ruler; you. See [[welcome]].
