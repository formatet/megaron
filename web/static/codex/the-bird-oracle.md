The Bird Oracle is a prayer to [[asterion]], and the only way to see far away without a person of yours standing there.

Name **a place on your map**: a city whose name you know (your own, or one of another Wanax that you remember), or a **named land** such as an island or a cape whose anchor you know. You cannot name a hex that has no name, nor a place you have never seen or been told of. Asterion's birds then go out over that place for **one game day**, and you see everything within **2 hexes** of it **live**, as if your own people stood there: armies, fleets and [[transfers|caravans]], each with its type, its size and its [[stances|stance]], just as for any [[sight|live eye]]. Nothing farther, and the mountains give no extra reach.

When the day is over the place is **memory** like any other: grey, frozen as you saw it, with the cities in it as you last saw them ([[foreign-cities]]). What the birds saw is written into your memory even if you never open the map that day.

**The one who is watched is told nothing**: no notice, no omen. It is sight, not a prayer against anyone.

It is dear, and it asks for good standing: a standing of 30 [[kharis]] or more, more than the everyday prayers. A richer gift does no more; it only makes the temple rest longer.

In **Kult → Prayers** choose the place from the list of cities and named places, then offer. In Keryx, `keryx rite --list` shows what you may name, and `keryx rite --prayer minoan_asterion_bird_oracle --target "<name>"` casts it; `keryx map` and `keryx hex` then show what the birds see during that game day.
