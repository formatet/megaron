Fruitful Season is a prayer to [[potnia]].

For 20 game days with the traditional gift, the people of the blessed city grow **half as fast again**, and its herd bears **half as many calves again** (rounded down, never fewer). Only growth is helped: a hungry city still shrinks, and a city that cannot feed more people does not grow. A larger gift makes the blessing last longer.

| Who is blessed | Gift |
|---|---|
| The city where you pray | twice the traditional offering |
| Every city of your realm | four times the traditional offering |

The same city cannot be blessed twice at once; a blessing on the whole realm may be laid over one on a single city. Calves bought with [[grain|grain]] still cost the grain they cost before; the extra calves are the blessing's. See [[growth]] and [[livestock]].
