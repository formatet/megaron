Honest notes on the current build, so you do not mistake a gap for your own mistake.

## Things the browser cannot do yet

Some orders exist in the game but have no button in the browser yet. They can be given from the command-line client ([[keryx]]):

- **Sacking, burning or annexing an occupied city** — `keryx occupation` ([[occupation]]).
- **Sending silver to one of your own colonies as a gift** — `keryx gift`.
- **Setting when a unit retreats in battle** — `keryx unit retreat-order`.
- **Changing your password** — `keryx password`.
- **Exploring unknown land with a land unit** — march step by step towards the unknown instead ([[sight]]).

## Things that work, but awkwardly

- **War → Movements does not show ordinary marches.** Follow your units in **War → Army** and on the map; incoming enemies arrive as *foreign march sighted* notifications ([[defence]]).
- **A march shows its travel time only after you send it** — the order menu does not forecast it.
- **March and Redirect in the Army tab take typed coordinates**, not a map click. Right-clicking the map is the friendlier path ([[marching]]).
- **Standing-order goods are typed as text** (`grain:200,fish:50`), and the route list does not show what a route carries ([[routes]]).
- **Search does not find messages or rumours**, despite its placeholder.
- **A few Swedish words survive** in the interface: *Visa →* means *Show*, *⚓ i hamn* means *in harbour*.

## Not in this version

**Kingdoms** — formal alliances, elected leaders, shared armies — are built but switched off, and will return once the foundations are proven. For now, diplomacy is [[messengers|letters]] and [[trade]].
