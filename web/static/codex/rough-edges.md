Honest notes on the current build, so you do not mistake a gap for your own mistake.

## Things that work, but awkwardly

- **A march shows its travel time only after you send it** — the order menu does not forecast it.
- **March in the Army tab takes typed coordinates**, not a map click. Right-clicking the map is the friendlier path — it also reaches units already on the march ([[marching]]).
- **Standing-order goods are typed as text** (`grain:200,fish:50`), and the route list does not show what a route carries ([[routes]]).
- **Search does not find messages or rumours**, despite its placeholder.

## Not in this version

**Kingdoms** — formal alliances, elected leaders, shared armies — are built but switched off, and will return once the foundations are proven. For now, diplomacy is [[messengers|letters]] and [[trade]].

Found something that is not on this list? [[report|Report it.]]
