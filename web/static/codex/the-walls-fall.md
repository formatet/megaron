The Walls Fall is a prayer to [[potnia]], and the only one that reaches out to another Wanax.

Name **a city of another Wanax** that is on your map and whose **wall you remember** (level 1 or more), and Potnia listens at once. **Three game days later** one wall level of that city falls: from level 3 to 2, from 2 to 1, from 1 to none. Two such prayers, said against the same city, bring down two levels. A city with no wall left loses nothing, though the gift is taken.

What you are told is only that **Potnia heard you**. You are not told whether the wall fell, nor what level it has now. You learn it by **seeing the city again**: a new sight updates your memory of it, as always ([[foreign-cities]], [[sight]]). The prayer works from what you remember, not from what is true now, so a wall you remember bare cannot be named, and a city you do not know cannot be named at all. It cannot be your own city.

A battle already under way at the walls is not changed: it fights on with the wall it began with. Only battles that begin after the wall has fallen see the lower level ([[battle]], [[sieges]], [[walls]]).

**The other Wanax** is told when the wall falls, always: "[[gods|the gods]] struck it", and the new level. A Wanax who stands well with the gods (a standing of 60 [[kharis]] or more) is also warned when you pray: Potnia's wrath is gathering over the walls of that city. Neither notice says who prayed, nor from where. See [[wrath-over-the-walls]].

A richer gift does no more. The traditional gift is dear, because it can cost someone else a great deal.

Offer in **Kult → Prayers** at a city with a [[temple]] of Potnia: choose the city from the list. In Keryx, `keryx rite --prayer minoan_potnia_walls_fall --target "<city>"`, and `keryx rite --list` shows the cities you can name.
