Everything your cities make, eat and trade is a **good**. The **Economy (⚖)** drawer shows your goods across every settlement, in three tabs: **Goods**, **Transfer** and **Routes & cargo**.

| Good | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Grain, fish, oil** | Equal food goods, eaten in proportion to availability ([[food]]). Oil also serves as an offering for [[rites|prayers]]. |
| **Wine** | Not food. Grown only on hills; the drink of the gods and the feast, offered in [[rites|prayers]] ([[wine]]). |

| Good | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Livestock** | Breeds itself in the pen (a calf per ten animals, fed with grain). Each animal is 3 food; kept as emergency food for the people when the four food goods do not cover their meal ([[food]]). |
