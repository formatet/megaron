The world advances one **game day** at a time. Durations use **game days**. Arrival and completion times use **your local clock**. The same number of game days can take different real time in different worlds.

## Game days and real time {#time-game-days-and-the-clock}

A march might take **8 game days**, with an estimate such as **today 18:34** beside it. Food supplies show how many game days they will last. Estimates follow the world's current pace and can change if it pauses or its pace changes.

The top bar shows the world's calendar; the sun and moon show the time in the world.

## A world that keeps running {#time-the-world-does-not-wait-for-you}

Production, meals, [[upkeep]], marches and [[battle|battles]] continue while you are away. Leave enough [[food]] and check [[coming-back|Notifications]] when you return.

## Orders before world start {#time-before-the-world-begins}

A new world waits for enough Wanaxes to join. You can look around, but orders become available when it starts. See [[getting-in]].
