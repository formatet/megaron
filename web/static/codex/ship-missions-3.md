- **Supplies.** Rowers eat the same per-person ration as spearmen, doubled while marching, posted or freighting ([[upkeep]]). The actual surviving crew determines the ration, and any carried soldiers need their own field rations too. A crew eats grain, fish, oil and wine, with the same proportional sharing as the city. The port loads food for the voyage from these four goods; livestock is not ship provision. War → Forces shows food for N game days and the goods aboard, including ships carrying goods on a freight route and waiting between its legs. Keryx `unit list` shows the same supply. Unused provisions come home as the goods that were loaded, so fish does not turn into grain. A ship turns home before its food runs too low for the voyage back, unless it can reach a city for provisions. You hear why when it reaches home; see [[ship-food-return]]. Ports feed visiting ships as guests; see [[xenia]]. At sea it eats only what was loaded aboard, never from a city's granary. The food gauge counts whole game days; "less than a game day" means the last ration is being eaten, "out of food" means nothing is left.
