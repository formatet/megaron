**Maquis, hills and river valleys also give timber:** each hex has room for one timber worker, producing **0.33 timber per game day**. A [[buildings|Lumbermill]] adds four places, making room for five timber workers, and multiplies each worker's output by ×1.7 at level 1, ×2.4 at level 2 or ×3.1 at level 3. Olive groves remain a richer source of timber; cedar forest produces cedar, which can cover timber costs.

On the map, a city's catchment is outlined in gold when you have that city in focus: when its City drawer is open, or when you click the city itself.
