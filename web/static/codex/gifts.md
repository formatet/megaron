A **gift or tribute** sends goods or silver to another Wanax's city, with nothing requested in return. It needs no acceptance. The city must be contacted, just as for a [[messengers|letter]].

Open **Economy → Transfer**. Choose your sending city, a destination marked with its Wanax's name **(gift)**, a good and a quantity, then **Send →**. In Keryx, use `keryx transfer --good silver --qty 20 --dest CityName`; use the settlement ID if two cities share a name. Your own cities remain [[transfers|internal transfers]].

## On the road

The goods leave your stock when sent. They travel in a physical caravan, or aboard a real free ship from your port when a sea route is used. Cargo can be intercepted. A ship remains bound until its empty voyage home finishes. A gift carries the same risk as any other cargo: on land only raiders, at sea also [[sea|storms]].

A ship damaged in interception keeps the existing [[transfers|naval interception outcome]]: half the cargo is lost, half sails home. The gift notice separates the exact quantities **lost** and **sent home**. This is a physical damaged return, not an immediate refund; that voyage can itself be intercepted.

## At the destination

The city receives only what fits in its store. Anything that does not fit is lost. **GiftDelivered** or **GiftLost** tells you the exact quantities received and lost. There is no return of goods.

If the city changes hands during the journey, its **new owner receives the gift**. The sender, intended recipient and actual recipient are notified. If the city has fallen or has no owner, the gift is lost.

A gift appears as a row among the letters in **Diplomacy → Threads**. The sender can see its departure; the recipient sees the outcome when delivery is resolved. It creates no trade offer and asks for nothing in return. Notifications and Keryx show the same delivery outcome.

**Gift from capital** to your own colony is a separate [[transfers|loyalty gesture]].
