The Scapegoat is a prayer to [[potnia]].

A goat is chosen to stand for your whole realm for 30 game days. If [[gods|the gods]] would **punish** any of your cities in that time, the goat bears it instead: **nothing is lost**, and the gods' mercy holds just as after any punishment — you are protected from further divine punishment for the same stretch. The goat is then spent. The prayer does **not** lower the chance of a punishment; it only changes what the punishment costs. If no punishment comes, the goat is simply gone after 30 game days.

You are told when the goat has borne a punishment and what it would have taken. It is said for the realm only, at four times the traditional offering, and only one goat can stand at a time. A larger gift does nothing more.
