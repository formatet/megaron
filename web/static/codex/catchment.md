A settlement lives off the land around it. That land is its **catchment**: the city's own hex plus the ring of hexes within **two** steps of it — eighteen hexes of land and water that its people can work. <!-- src: internal/hexgrid CatchmentRadius -->

The catchment is the **only** place production comes from. There is no abstract "income": every sack of grain, every log and every ingot was produced by one of your [[citizens]] standing on one of these hexes, or working in a [[buildings|building]] in the city.

## What it means for you

- **Where you found decides what you can make.** A catchment of plains and coast feeds people; one with a mountain may hold copper or silver. The [[founding]] forecast shows you the catchment before you commit.
- **Catchments may not overlap.** Two settlements cannot claim the same ground; the game tells you how far to move.
- **A hex you have not scouted cannot be worked.** It is listed below the grid rather than drawn.
- **An enemy on your catchment hurts.** A hostile unit in [[stances|sentry]] on one of your hexes stops that hex feeding you. Hold enough of them long enough and a city falls without a battle — see [[sieges]].
