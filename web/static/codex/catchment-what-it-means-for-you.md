- **Where you found decides what you can make.** A catchment of plains and coast feeds people; one with a mountain may hold copper or silver. The [[founding]] forecast shows you the catchment before you commit.

- **Neighbours share ground.** Cities may be founded as close as **three** hexes apart, so their catchments overlap. A hex belongs to whichever city has [[citizens]] working on it; a neighbour cannot place anyone there while you hold it.

- **A held hex can be taken.** Stand one of your own forces on it in [[stances|fortify or sentry]], then place a citizen there. The holder's citizens go home to their idle pool, their building on the hex becomes yours at the same level, and any build they had queued there is cancelled and refunded. They are told by dispatch. A unit that merely marches through takes nothing, and if the holder has a unit standing guard there too, you must defeat it first ([[battle]]).

- **They can take it back** the same way: a unit in fortify or sentry, then a citizen placed.
