Every ship needs a **Harbour** and a **Shipyard** ([[buildings]]). There are three kinds ([[units|Forces]]):

- **Galley** — 30 timber, no silver to build or maintain. Transport and sight at sea. Can carry goods.
- **War Galley** — the warship. Needs cedar and a Foundry. Never carries cargo.
- **Emporos** — a trade hull built with 90 cedar, no timber or silver. It draws no silver pay. It is no warship (1 die in battle, like a Galley), but carries more than a Galley.

A [[transfers|transfer]] or [[routes|standing order]] that goes by sea needs a free Galley or Emporos waiting in the sending port — the War Galley is never chosen. With automatic routing, no free hull means land instead if a route exists, or refusal. An explicitly chosen sea route never switches to land.
