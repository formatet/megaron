The Thread is a prayer to [[asterion]].

Land [[transfers|caravans]] you name can only be taken by a hostile sentry standing **within one hex** of them, not two, for 12 game days. It holds also for a caravan already on the road. Without the prayer a [[stances|sentry]] two hexes from the road can stop a caravan; with it, the sentry must be right beside it. You choose **which caravans**; the wider the blessing, the dearer the gift.

| Who is blessed | Gift |
|---|---|
| One named land caravan of yours, on the road now | the traditional offering |
| Every land caravan that leaves the city where you pray, now and while the prayer lasts | twice the traditional offering |
| Every land caravan of your realm, now and while the prayer lasts | four times the traditional offering |

The prayer is read each time the road is watched, not when a caravan leaves: a caravan under way is protected from the next watch, and loses the protection the moment the prayer runs out. It does not hide a caravan and it does not stop a sentry that is beside it. [[runners|Runners]] and [[messengers]] are never taken, so they need no thread, and ships are not helped (see [[sea]]). Only your own caravans can be named, and the same caravan cannot be blessed twice at once. A larger gift lasts longer, as with other lasting prayers, measured against the gift for the step you chose. There is no step for ever.

Your caravan list says "the Thread" with the game days left. Others do not see it.

Offer in **Kult → Prayers** at the matching city and god: pick *One named*, *This city's* or *The whole realm*, and for one caravan choose it from the list. In Keryx, `keryx rite --prayer minoan_asterion_thread_v2 --scope unit --target "<name>"`, or `--scope city` / `--scope realm`; `keryx cargo` shows the Thread beside the caravan it covers.

The older **The Thread Shows a Path**, a gift for the next departure, can no longer be offered.
