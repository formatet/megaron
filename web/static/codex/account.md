Click your Wanax name at the top left to open **Account**. Its realm summary has five rows for your selected world: your Wanax name, founding day, owned active cities, their total population, and your realm's Kharis.

**Since today 18:34**, or another clock time and date, marks the founding of your current life. If you have risen and started again, it follows that new life. A Host that has not founded its capital shows **Not yet founded**. The population is the population of your cities; it does not include your travelling Host. No other ruler's cities or statistics appear here.

From [[keryx]], use `keryx me`, or `keryx me --json`. The API is `GET /api/v1/worlds/{world}/me` with your Bearer token. It returns only your own realm in that world.

The same account window lets you change your password, open [[community-chat]], and sign out. To delete your account, write to the admin in the community chat.
