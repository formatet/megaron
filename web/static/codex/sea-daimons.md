The Sea Daimons of Stillness are not persons but forces: wind, wave and the deep. Their temple opens the prayer for calm water.

Build a temple for this god in **City → Buildings → Construct** or use `keryx build --type temple --god sea_daimons_stillness`. [[temple]] workers and daily offerings maintain realm-wide [[kharis]].

Prayers: [[favourable-passage]].
