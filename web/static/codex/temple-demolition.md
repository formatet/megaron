Voluntarily demolishing a [[temples|temple]] angers its god. Your realm loses **10 kharis per temple level**, down to a **floor of 1**. This is an immediate consequence of your own demolition, separate from the daily chance of [[divine-wrath|divine punishment]]. It does not grant punishment protection.

Before you confirm, **City → Demolish** and `keryx demolish --type temple --god potnia` show the actual kharis loss, standing afterward and the realm ceiling before and after. Keryx asks for confirmation; non-interactive or `--json` use requires `--yes`. The result and the **god is angered** notification record what actually happened. Nothing is refunded, and only that temple's workers become idle.

Your best remaining temple determines the ceiling: no temple **40**, level 1 **50**, level 2 **75**, level 3 **100**. Another equally high temple can keep the ceiling unchanged. **A lower ceiling stops growth; it does not cut existing kharis.** Standing above it declines by ordinary daily cult upkeep; temple gains do not offset this decline. Once standing is at or below the ceiling, ordinary gain and upkeep apply again. The voluntary demolition loss still applies.

A temple destroyed by **plundering** creates **no demolition debt** for its owner. Its loss can lower the ceiling and remove its production. Cancelling construction is not demolition and does not incur the kharis loss.
