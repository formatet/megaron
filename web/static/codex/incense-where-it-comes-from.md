Incense is found only on marked hexes of dry scrub (maquis) and semi-desert — the ground that gives almost nothing else. A marked hex shows a small amber drop on the map and the word **Incense** in its inspect panel. [[rites|Potnia's Vision]] can mark an incense source you neither see nor remember; it creates memory, never living eyes. `keryx cities` includes incense among the deposits of known cities. A deposit in your [[catchment]] needs no building: **two citizens** placed on the hex ([[citizens]]) gather it. There are about ten such places in a world of the usual size, scattered over both halves of the map and more of them in the east. Most Wanaxes will have none, which is why it is carried and traded ([[trade]]).
