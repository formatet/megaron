A Market gives your city a **market board**: one line saying what the city buys and one saying what it sells, up to three goods each. You write it yourself, and it says only what you choose. Nothing checks it against your stores, and it can be empty.

Other rulers read the board on the spot. Their [[transfers|caravans]], armies and ships read it when they pass within reach of the market, and their [[messengers|messengers]] read it when they arrive in the city. A level 1 Market reaches as far as the city's own fields, and each higher level reaches further. What they read stays with their ruler as it stood then. If you change the board later, they see the change only the next time someone of theirs passes.

A ruler whose caravan reads your board from a distance learns where your city lies. They can write to you, but before they can send you a [[trade|trade offer]] their own people must reach the city. The board does not trade for you; it tells others whom to ask.

Write it in **City → Buildings → Market board**, or with `keryx market-board <city> --buys tin --sells wine`. The Market needs no workers.

Build or upgrade it in **City → Buildings → Construct**, or use `keryx build --type market`.
