A Market gives your city a **market board**: one line saying what the city buys and one saying what it sells, up to three goods each. [[silver|Silver]] may go on the board too — *Sells silver* tells others you pay for goods — even though a [[trade|trade offer]] always has silver on the other side. You write it yourself, and it says only what you choose. Nothing checks it against your stores, and it can be empty.

Other rulers read the board on the spot, and only there: whoever of theirs reaches the city reads it — a [[messengers|messenger]] arriving with a letter, a [[transfers|caravan]], an army or a ship whose road runs through the city. Passing nearby is not enough. A board shows only what its ruler chose to write. What they read stays with their ruler as it stood then. If you change the board later, they see the change only the next time someone of theirs passes.

The board does not trade for you; it tells others whom to ask.

Write it in **City → Buildings → Market board**, or with `keryx market-board <city> --buys tin --sells wine`. The Market needs no workers.

Build it in **City → Buildings → Construct**, or use `keryx build --type market`.
