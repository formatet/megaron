Soldiers need two things every day: **pay** and **bread**.

## Pay — silver

Every soldier draws pay in [[silver]] every day. If you cannot pay, you are warned first; if it goes on, **men desert**, and a unit that loses everyone is gone. Recruit only what your silver can carry.

## Bread — grain

Soldiers eat like the rest of your people ([[food]]). A unit in garrison eats from its city. **A unit in the field eats double**, and there is nothing to forage — so a long march, a siege line or a posted watch is a real cost ([[stances]]). <!-- src: server/internal/combat/upkeep.go upkeepFieldGrainFactor -->

A unit that runs out of food **starves**: it loses men each day, and can starve to nothing. A ship whose crew runs short turns for home by itself, and says so.

## Before you settle

Your [[horde|Nomadic Host]]'s escort is paid from the store you carry. When that silver runs out, the escort starts to desert — which is why the Host panel counts it down.

## The rule of thumb

Your army is only as large as your economy can feed and pay. Build [[silver]] income and [[food]] surplus before you build cohorts ([[units]]).
