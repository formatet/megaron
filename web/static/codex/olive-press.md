An Olive Press refines [[oil|oil]] from the city’s groves.

**A press refines only what grove workers bring in.** Oil comes from two kinds of work together: [[citizens]] placed on olive-grove hexes in the city’s [[catchment]], and citizens working the press itself. The oil you get is limited by the weaker of the two. A press shown as **FULL** (`keryx staff olive_press`) only means its places are staffed, not that it is producing: with no one on the groves it adds nothing beyond the baseline oil. See [[oil]].

Build or upgrade it in **City → Buildings → Construct**, or use `keryx build --type olive_press`.
