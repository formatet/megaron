Every unit you own, with its state and current grain and silver upkeep per game day in plain words. **March** is shown while it can move; a damaged land cohort garrisoned in its **original home city** can also **Reinforce** — another city you own does not qualify. A land unit on the march shows **Recall**. The card also shows stance and **Set**, battle retreat, **Redirect**, **Fetch by ship**, **Load** / **Unload** or **Repair**, whenever that unit can use them. Choose **Destination city** in a march or redirect form to target a known city quickly; map clicks remain available. Keryx accepts `--to "City name"` on march, post and redirect. See [[marching]], [[stances]] and [[sea]].

**Marches** lists your forces on the march (**Your Marches**) and every foreign unit you can see marching on one of your settlements (**Incoming**), soonest arrival first. You only see an incoming column once one of your eyes has it in sight — the *foreign march sighted* notification fires at the same moment ([[defence]]).

**Forces on a hex:** select their current hex on the map to inspect the same force cards, your own first. A marching force appears where it is now. Ships show hull, passengers and available provision information; orders and their destination remain visible. A pending messenger is still carrying an order, which takes effect when delivered. **View in Forces** opens the selected force. **Redirect** selects that force and asks for a new destination; **Recall** sends its existing return order. Ships on missions at sea take neither command. On a narrow screen, expand a force's row for details. Your own cities continue to open the City drawer.

`keryx hex <q,r>` lists the forces at that coordinate and the corresponding commands. A foreign force shows only observed information: owner, kind, strength, status, heading and any reported passengers. Its hull, provisions, orders and actual destination are unknown; a movement that *seems* bound for one of your cities remains an estimate. See [[map-actors]].
