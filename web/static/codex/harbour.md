A Harbour opens deep-water fishing places in a coastal city’s [[catchment]]. Build it through **City → Buildings → Construct**, or `keryx build --type harbour`. Place fishers on the water itself; the harbour has no workplaces to staff. See [[deep-water-fishing]] for its fishing places and [[shipyard]] for building ships.
