The **Sitos** is your city's public granary: the store that stands between a bad month and a famine.

Find it in **City → Production → More → Food reserve**: grain made against grain eaten, the city's food coverage, what the reserve holds, and the thresholds for storing and releasing. The primary **Food** line reports how long the city's current grain and fish stocks last at current consumption; the reserve is shown separately under More. The **State** row tells you in words what the granary is doing:

- *storing away a share of the surplus* — a good year; the granary is filling;
- *releasing food to the city* — the city is eating more than it makes, and the granary is covering the gap;
- *resting* — nothing to store and nothing needed;
- *EMPTY and the stock is shrinking — the city is unprotected* — **the one to act on.** The next shortfall will cost lives. See [[food]].

When the granary releases food you get a notification, and it says so if the granary is now empty.

The granary holds food only. Silver never passes through it.

A well-filled Sitos buys time under [[sieges|siege]].
