Potnia, **the Mistress**, is the mother of animals and of the sea. Her temple opens prayers that show you hidden metal, strengthen a city under attack, make people and herds grow, take a punishment on a goat, and bring down the wall of another Wanax's city.

Build a temple for this god in **City → Buildings → Construct** or use `keryx build --type temple --god potnia`. [[temple]] workers and daily offerings maintain realm-wide [[kharis]].

Prayers: [[potnias-vision]], [[mistress-of-the-walls]], [[fruitful-season]], [[the-scapegoat]], [[the-walls-fall]].
