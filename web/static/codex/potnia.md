Potnia, **the Mistress**, is the mother of animals and of the sea. Her temple opens two prayers: one that shows you hidden metal, and one that strengthens a city under attack.

Build a temple for this god in **City → Buildings → Construct** or use `keryx build --type temple --god potnia`. [[temple]] workers and daily offerings maintain realm-wide [[kharis]].

Prayers: [[potnias-vision]], [[mistress-of-the-walls]].
