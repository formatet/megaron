An empty ship in your own active Harbour can be **Laid up** from its Forces card. Its crew returns to the port's population and can work there; the hull draws no food or silver and remains in port. The card says **Laid up** and offers **Fit out**. Fitting out takes the ship's full crew from that city's current population and prepares it for **three game days**. It cannot sail or be cancelled during that time, and its crew draws home-rate food and, for a War Galley, half field pay from the start. If the city is annexed, its laid-up and fitting-out ships go with the city. Disbanding a laid-up ship removes the hull without returning the same crew twice; choose the particular hull to disband by its name. Keryx: `unit lay-up --unit ID` and `unit fit-out --unit ID`.
