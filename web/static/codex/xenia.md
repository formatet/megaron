The gods keep an order among the Wanaxes even when they are rivals. The Greeks of a later age called it **themis**: what is right because the gods hold it so. Its oldest part is **xenia**, the bond between host and guest.

## Themis: what the gods hold

A few things are not yours to choose. They are not treaties and no one signs them; they are how the world works.

## The messenger is sacred

A herald cannot be attacked. No army, raider or Wanax can intercept a messenger, on the way out or on the way home. A letter always reaches its city unless the sea itself takes the runner. See [[messengers]].

## We carry each other's messengers

A runner who reaches a harbour without a ship of his own boards whatever ship sails his way, **yours or another Wanax's**. No one refuses a herald passage. See [[messengers]] and [[sea]].

## The guest is fed

When a ship reaches a city, its own or another Wanax's, the city gives it food for the voyage home. It is free and needs no consent. The port first keeps enough for its own people's daily meal and gives only what it can spare. See [[ship-food-return]].

## The suppliant: hiketeia

When a Wanax's last city falls and they [[last-city|rise again]], their survivors are suppliants, under the gods' protection until they found a city. You may still attack their Nomadic Host or its escort. If a battle against them actually begins, **your realm loses 70 kharis** (never below the floor of 1). A march that never reaches them costs nothing. Once they have founded a city, the protection ends.

Before an order against a host you can see goes out, you are warned: in the march panel, **March anyway** sends it; in [[keryx|Keryx]], add `--confirm` to `keryx unit march`. No one else is told; your own kharis shows the loss in **Kult**.
