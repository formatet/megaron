Join the [Megaron Discord](https://discord.gg/NxtftQUMYW) for questions, stories and alpha news. The chat is optional and outside the game; send in-world letters through [[messengers]].

## Joining Discord {#community-chat-your-chat-account}

Use your Discord account to join. It is separate from your Megaron account.

## Community rules and privacy {#community-chat-chat-rules-and-privacy}

- Be decent. No harassment, no hate, nothing illegal.
- Talk about the game, not other players’ private lives.
- Keep personal information private.
- Contact a moderator if something in the community needs attention.

Discord is a separate service with its own account and privacy settings. For a problem inside the game, use [[report]].
