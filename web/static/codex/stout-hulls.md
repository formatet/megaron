Stout Hulls is a prayer to the [[sea-daimons|Sea Daimons]] of Stillness.

Each ship you name takes the next [[storms|storm]] it meets **unharmed**, within 12 game days of the prayer. The following storm strikes in full. You choose **who** is blessed; the wider the blessing, the dearer the gift.

| Who is blessed | Gift |
|---|---|
| One named ship of yours | the traditional offering |
| Every ship supported by the city where you pray | twice the traditional offering |
| Every ship in your realm | four times the traditional offering |
| **One named ship, for ever** | **twenty times** the traditional offering |

Every blessed ship carries its own ward, so a blessing over several ships protects each of them once. The blessing is read when the storm is met, wherever the ship is, and a storm met before the prayer was said is not covered. The storm notice says the ship was shielded (hull unchanged). The ward replaces [[gods|the gods]]' own favour or wrath for that storm. Only your own ships can be named. The same ships cannot be blessed twice at once.

**For ever.** At twenty times the gift the Sea Daimons lay **one plank more** in a named ship, for good: her hull is now 6 instead of 5, and she is given the extra plank at once (a full ship stands at 6/6). Every storm still takes 2 hull and a ship at 0 still founders, so she is never unsinkable, only able to take one more blow. A [[shipyard]] repairs her back to 6, not 5. In [[battle]] she rolls as many dice when full as any full ship, but lasts longer. The prayer cannot be said twice over the same ship, and the temple rests as after an ordinary prayer. Your force list shows her hull as 6/6.

Offer in **Kult → Prayers** at the matching city and god: pick *One named*, *This city's*, *The whole realm* or *For ever*, and for one ship choose it from the list. In Keryx, `keryx rite --prayer minoan_stillness_hull --scope unit --target "<name>"`, or `--scope city` / `--scope realm`, or `--scope permanent --target "<name>"` for the extra plank.
