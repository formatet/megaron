Stout Hulls is a prayer to the [[sea-daimons|Sea Daimons]] of Stillness.

Each ship you name takes the next [[storms|storm]] it meets **unharmed**, within 12 game days of the prayer. The following storm strikes in full. You choose **who** is blessed; the wider the blessing, the dearer the gift.

| Who is blessed | Gift |
|---|---|
| One named ship of yours | the traditional offering |
| Every ship supported by the city where you pray | twice the traditional offering |
| Every ship in your realm | four times the traditional offering |

Every blessed ship carries its own ward, so a blessing over several ships protects each of them once. The blessing is read when the storm is met, wherever the ship is, and a storm met before the prayer was said is not covered. The storm notice says the ship was shielded (hull unchanged). The ward replaces [[gods|the gods]]' own favour or wrath for that storm. Only your own ships can be named. The same ships cannot be blessed twice at once.

Offer in **Kult → Prayers** at the matching city and god: pick *One named*, *This city's* or *The whole realm*, and for one ship choose it from the list. In Keryx, `keryx rite --prayer minoan_stillness_hull --scope unit --target "<name>"`, or `--scope city` / `--scope realm`.
