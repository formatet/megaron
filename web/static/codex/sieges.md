## Starving a city out

You do not need to break a wall. Put units in **sentry** ([[stances]]) on the hexes a city works, and those hexes stop feeding it. Hold that long enough, with the city's food need uncovered, and **it capitulates on its own** — without a battle. The wall is never breached; the belly gives up.

This is the most powerful thing in the game that needs no [[bronze]], and the reason a city that cannot feed itself from its own [[catchment]] is fragile however thick its walls are.

## Blockaded hexes

A hex held by a hostile watch is **blockaded**: your people standing there produce nothing for you while it lasts. You are told when a hex is blockaded and when it is freed.

## When it happens to you

You get a notification when a siege begins and when it is lifted. The City drawer carries a red banner naming who holds the approaches, and the map draws a dark cloud over the city.

What helps:

- a full [[sitos|granary]] — the city eats from it while the fields are cut off;
- food brought in from outside by [[transfers|transfer]] — if it can get through;
- a relief force that drives the besiegers off the hexes ([[battle]]).

If the city falls, see [[occupation]].
