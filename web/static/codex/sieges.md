A siege threatens a city by holding the ground that feeds it.

## Starving a city out {#sieges-starving-a-city-out}

You do not need to break a wall. Put forces in **sentry** ([[stances]]) on the hexes a city works, and those hexes stop feeding it. Hold it until the city's food need has gone uncovered for **30 game days in a row**, and **it capitulates on its own** — without a battle. The wall is never breached; the belly gives up.

This is the most powerful thing in the game that needs no [[bronze]], and the reason a city that cannot feed itself from its own [[catchment]] is fragile however thick its walls are.

## Blockaded catchment hexes {#sieges-blockaded-hexes}

A hex held by a hostile watch is **blockaded**: your people standing there produce nothing for you while it lasts. You are told when a hex is blockaded and when it is freed.

## Taking a catchment hex {#sieges-taking-a-hex}

Where two cities' [[catchment|catchments]] overlap, the hex belongs to the city with [[citizens|citizens]] on it. A rival with a unit of their own in [[stances|fortify or sentry]] there can take it by placing a citizen: your citizens on it go home to your idle pool, your building on it becomes theirs at the same level, and a build you had queued there is cancelled and refunded. You get a **HexTaken** notification naming who took it and how many of your citizens are idle. Take it back the same way. If your own unit is standing guard on the hex, it must be defeated first.

## Responding to a siege {#sieges-when-it-happens-to-you}

You get a notification when a siege begins and when it is lifted. The City drawer carries a red banner naming who holds the approaches, and the map draws a dark cloud over the city.

What helps:

- full [[food|city food stores]] — the city eats its [[grain|grain]], [[fish|fish]] and [[oil|oil]] while the fields are cut off;
- food brought in from outside by [[transfers|transfer]] — if it can get through;
- a relief force that drives the besiegers off the hexes ([[battle]]).

If the city falls, see [[occupation]].
