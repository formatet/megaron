**Left-click your own city** and the City drawer opens on it. If you hold several settlements, the **‹** and **›** arrows in the header cycle between them; your capital is marked **★**.

The drawer has four tabs: **Production**, **Warehouse**, **Buildings** and **Garrison**. If your city is under siege, a red banner at the top names who holds the approaches ([[sieges]]).

## Production — the dashboard

Three sections show the essentials:

- **Population** — whole citizen groups, those free to work, and [[livestock|livestock]]. One citizen represents 100 people; fewer than 100 remaining people do not make a free worker. The exact population is under **More**.
- **[[catchment]] & workplaces** — the placement grid, where you put people to work. See [[citizens]].
- **Food** — today’s food net per game day, and whether the fields can feed everyone. See [[food]].

Open **More** for people in service and the labour pool, **Devotion** ([[temples]]), **Food details** ([[food]]), **Last game day**, and **Daily history → Show recent game days**, which shows the books for the last game day and then the events of each recent game day. For every good the books list what really happened to the store, one line per cause: production, citizens' meals, army rations and wages, each temple's tithe, calves, gifts and trade, goods lost because the store was full, and so on. A line called **Other** appears only when a change of the store has no cause listed; it should normally be absent.

## Warehouse

What the city holds and how much room it has: **Food** (how many game days the stocks last, and each food against its [[granary]] cap), the **Herd** against its [[pen]] cap, and every other good alphabetically, with its cap where it has one. A store at its cap is marked **full**.

## Buildings

What stands, what is queued (with a **✕** to cancel), forces in training, and **Construct**, a list of buildings with a **Build** button each. See [[buildings]].

## Garrison

The forces standing in this city. Orders to them are given from **War** ([[units|Forces]]); **Recruit** beside the title opens War on its Recruit tab for this city.
