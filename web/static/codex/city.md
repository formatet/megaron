**Left-click your own city** and the City drawer opens on it. If you hold several settlements, the **‹** and **›** arrows in the header cycle between them; your capital is marked **★**.

The drawer has three tabs: **Production**, **Buildings** and **Garrison**. If your city is under siege, a red banner at the top names who holds the approaches ([[sieges]]).

## Production — the dashboard

Three sections show the essentials:

- **Population** — people, those free to work, and livestock. **Slaughter** trades one animal for ten people at once.
- **Catchment & workplaces** — the placement grid, where you put people to work. See [[citizens]].
- **Food** — how many days the city's current grain and fish stocks last at current consumption, whether stocks are growing, and whether the fields can feed everyone. See [[food]].

Open **More** for people in service and the labour pool, **Devotion** ([[temples]]), **Food reserve** ([[sitos]]), **Last day**, the **Loyalty log**, and **Daily history → Show recent days**. On colonies, **Gift from capital** is also here ([[transfers]]). These details and controls remain available while the main view stays short.

## Buildings

What stands, what is queued (with a **✕** to cancel), units in training, and **Construct**. See [[buildings]].

## Garrison

The units standing in this city. Orders to them are given from **War** ([[units]]).
