The temple rule has changed. Your dispatch records the realm’s demand and the weight its temples could give at the transition.

Demand is active cities squared. [[temple]] levels weigh 1/4/9, multiplied by staffing and the paid tithe share. See [[temples]] and [[kharis]].

Each active realm receives this dispatch **once**, with **14 game days of protection from divine punishment** to adjust its temples. Existing longer protection is preserved. Later restarts do not renew it. This protection does not prevent daily cult [[upkeep|upkeep]] or the kharis loss from voluntary temple demolition. Kult shows the current protection and when it ends.
