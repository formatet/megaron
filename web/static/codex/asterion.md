Asterion is **lord of the labyrinth**, the god of ways and of finding them. His temple opens prayers that speed [[transfers|caravans]] and [[runners|Runners]], harden troops at the opening of a [[battle]] and thin the sentries' reach over a road.

Build a temple for this god in **City → Buildings → Construct** or use `keryx build --type temple --god asterion`. [[temple]] workers and daily offerings maintain realm-wide [[kharis]].

Prayers: [[swift-feet]], [[the-bulls-horns]], [[the-thread]].

See [[temples]], [[rites]] and [[offerings]].
