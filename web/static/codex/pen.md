A Pen increases the herd a city can hold.

Build or upgrade it in **City → Buildings → Construct**, or use `keryx build --type pen`.

| Reference | Current rule |
|---|---|

| Build time, level 1 | 4 game days |
| Maximum level | 3 |
| Cost to level 2 | 12 timber (or cedar) · 8 stone |

See [[buildings]] and [[citizens]].

| Building | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Pen** | Raises the herd cap by 100 per level (50 without one). Animals above the cap are eaten as food, then lost ([[food]]); a full pen adds its overflow to the city's food net. Works inside the city. |
