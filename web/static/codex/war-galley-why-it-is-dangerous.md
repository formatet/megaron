A War Galley is not a thicker or better-armoured ship. It is dangerous because of who is aboard: rowers, archers, slingers and boarding fighters — a trained marine force, not ordinary oarsmen or soldiers carried as passengers. In a sea fight it shoots at the enemy from a distance, then closes to board or force a surrender. That is why it rolls 5 dice where a Galley or an Emporos rolls 1.

It is also why only the War Galley costs silver and draws pay. A Galley or an Emporos is rowed by ordinary men from the city; a War Galley keeps standing marines.

So it is a costly specialist. It strikes hard and is the fastest ship at sea, but it has the same 5 hull points as any other hull. Three Galleys roll fewer dice together, yet carry three times the hull — a real danger to it, more so once it is damaged.

Build it when ships must fight or hunt other ships. It carries no cargo, so trade stays with the [[galley]] and the [[emporos]]. See [[units|Forces]] and [[battle]].
