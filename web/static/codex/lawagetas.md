The **Lawagetas** is your war-leader and right hand. At the start of your reign he speaks from a small card at the bottom of the map: one line, the next step you have not yet taken, with how many are done (such as 2/4).

## The four steps {#lawagetas-the-four-steps}

1. **Find us a home.** Click your [[horde|Host]], then the land around it, and found where the land will feed you ([[founding]]).
2. **Put the idle hands to work**, food first. Every citizen without a place in your city counts ([[citizens]]).
3. **Order a building.** [[timber|Timber]] is usually what runs short ([[buildings]]). While it goes up, the card tells you when it is ready and to come back then.
4. **Send people out to look.** March a unit somewhere you have not seen ([[exploration]]).

The Lawagetas shows them in this order and checks them against your realm each time, so he never tells you to do what you have already done.

## When the advice ends {#lawagetas-when-it-ends}

His advice is only for the first steps. When all four are done he says one closing line and the card never returns. He gives no other advice and sends no notifications.

## Hiding and bringing him back {#lawagetas-hide-and-show}

Press ✕ on the card to hide it. To bring it back, open the Account window (click your Wanax name) and check **Show the Lawagetas' first steps**. In [[keryx]], `keryx steps` lists the four with a mark for each one done, `keryx steps --hide` hides the card and `keryx steps --show` brings it back.
