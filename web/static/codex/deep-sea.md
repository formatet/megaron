Deep sea is open navigable water; fishing there requires a [[harbour|Harbour]].

Select a known hex to inspect its ground and available production. City placement shows its actual workplaces and the next worker’s yield; movement previews show the route’s actual arrival time. In Keryx, inspect nearby known ground with `keryx map`.

See [[catchment]], [[citizens]] and [[marching]].

**[[galley|Galley]] base speed: 5 hexes per game day**, with a full crew and no soldiers aboard. [[rivers|River]] current, crew, carried troops and ship type can change the travel time; check the forecast. See [[ship-types]].

## Base production

Per hex, without buildings or mineral deposits. Each worker is one citizen group. The places shown are limits for each good; a worker does one job.
