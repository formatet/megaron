- **A hex you have not scouted cannot be worked.** It is listed below the grid rather than drawn.

- **An enemy on your catchment hurts.** A hostile unit in [[stances|sentry]] on one of your hexes stops that hex feeding you. Hold enough of them long enough and a city falls without a battle — see [[sieges]].

A hex shown as held by another city is already being worked by that city. Shared reach does not make its workers yours; the placement card explains whether you can take it.
