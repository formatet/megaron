Your people eat **grain, fish and oil** every game day. Wine is not food: it is the drink of the gods and the feast ([[wine]]). One unit of each supplies one unit of food. The meal is shared in proportion to what is available, including the game day’s production: a city with 100 grain and 900 fish takes one tenth of its meal as grain and nine tenths as fish. No food good is used up first. If these four cannot feed the people, livestock remains their emergency food: each animal supplies 3 food. Armies, ships and caravans use the four food goods, not livestock.
