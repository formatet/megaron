Megaron

Megaron is a slow strategy game set in a mythic Bronze Age eastern Mediterranean. You play a Wanax, a ruler, and build up settlements: feed them, put people to work, trade, raise and keep armies, explore, and deal with the gods and with other rulers.

The world keeps running while you are logged out. Production, travel and messages take time. You give orders, leave, and come back later to see what happened.

Everything travels by messenger

Nothing in Megaron is instant. An order to your own army is carried by a runner and takes effect when the runner gets there. A message to another ruler arrives when the messenger does, and the answer comes back the same way. Messengers are sacred and cannot be stopped. What you know about the world is what your own people have seen or been told.

No tech tree

There is no tech tree, and none is planned. Better units exist: elite infantry and chariots need bronze, war galleys need cedar. What limits them is what you can get hold of, not research. The game is more about who you talk to and what you can trade for than about which units you have.

What we are trying to build

A world where situations come out of what players decide and how the game's systems meet: trade, shortages, relationships. Some things you will only understand by trying them.

This is an early alpha

Much is unfinished. Balance is uneven, parts of the interface are unclear, and some things are broken. You don't need to understand everything at once. The rules and a reference are in the Codex, behind the ? in the top right corner.

What we want to hear

Concrete examples help most.

  • What was easy or hard to understand?
  • What were you trying to do, what did you expect, and what happened?
  • What was interesting? What was frustrating, boring or unclear?
  • Did you want to log in again later?
  • Bugs or strange results.

Use the Report button at the bottom right in the game, or keryx report if you play from the command line. Tell us too when the Codex is unclear, missing something, or doesn't match the game.
